# 02 — AI adoption roadmap: 30/60/90 days

**Illustrative plan; implementation depends on institutional approvals and technical feasibility.**

| Window | Programme management outputs | Responsible stakeholders | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30: Discover & protect | Baseline interviews; AI inventory; use-case register; AI policy gap review; risk register; sponsor/PMO governance; select up to 3 safe pilots | PMO, CIO/IT, DPO, Legal, HR/L&D, academic representatives | Charter signed; data guardrails approved; use-case owners named |
| Days 31–60: Pilot & learn | Pilot design, procurement/security clearance, vendor configuration, role-based literacy, consent and UAT, evaluation scorecards, feedback/incident route | Business owners, vendors, IT Security, champions | Test evidence, human oversight, quality thresholds and rollback approved |
| Days 61–90: Measure & decide | Adoption reporting, workflow quality review, unresolved-risk remediation, lessons learned, benefits validation, sustainment plan | PMO, finance, risk, executive sponsor | Scale/hold/stop decision documented with reasons |

## Workstream handoffs
- Intake -> risk classification -> business case -> steering shortlist.
- Steering shortlist -> procurement and security review -> solution design -> UAT.
- UAT -> policy/ethics sign-off -> controlled pilot -> telemetry and feedback.
- Pilot evidence -> finance-validated benefits -> scale/hold/retire.

## Example pilot requirements
### A. Staff policy assistant
- Ground answers exclusively in approved current policy documents; surface citations.
- Offer “I don't know / route to HR” instead of unsupported certainty.
- Keep access role-based and log use appropriately.
- In testing, reviewers check answer correctness, citation accuracy and disclosure failures.

### B. Student service triage
- Suggest a help-desk category and escalation; person makes final decision.
- No automated eligibility/admission/discipline decision.
- Test ambiguous requests, accessibility needs and multilingual cases.

### C. Meeting summaries
- Restrict capture to consented participants and approved channels.
- Require human check of names, commitments and sensitive details.
- Retention and redaction follow IT/DPO approvals.

## Example milestones
- **M1:** executive sponsorship and charter accepted.
- **M2:** pilot risk cases accepted.
- **M3:** pilot UAT and user preparation completed.
- **M4:** 90-day evidence review with go/no-go decisions.

No claimed achieved time savings or compliance outcomes; these are proposed gates only.
