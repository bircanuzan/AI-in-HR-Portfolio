# Project 47 — AI Adoption Program Management
## Institution-wide AI adoption, programme governance and measurable value

**Independent portfolio case study inspired by the responsibilities of an AI Adoption Programme Manager vacancy at Zayed University.** The case organisation is fictional (**Horizon University, UAE**) and all dates, roles, examples and metrics are illustrative. This is not a Zayed University project, employer engagement, live deployment or verified outcome.

### The challenge
A multi-campus university has several AI experiments, but no consistent programme governance, decision rights, workforce enablement, adoption measurement or incident escalation. The programme office must align use cases, security, ethics, vendors and stakeholders before controlled scaling.

### What this portfolio demonstrates
- Programme charter, delivery governance, scope and decision rights.
- A staged 30/60/90-day AI adoption roadmap linked to a programme management office (PMO).
- Cross-functional workstream coordination, RACI and vendor acceptance criteria.
- Responsible AI policy controls, risk tiers, human oversight and incident escalation.
- Role-based AI literacy, change communication and a champions network.
- KPI definitions, benefits hypotheses, reporting and synthetic dashboard inputs.

### Example use cases (fictional)
1. **Staff policy knowledge assistant** — search approved internal guidance with human verification; no sensitive records in an unapproved tool.
2. **Student services triage support** — suggest routing to humans; never autonomously decide admission, financial aid or disciplinary outcomes.
3. **Meeting summary assistant** — draft summaries and action logs with consent, restricted access and human approval.

### Deliverable acceptance tests
- Every workstream has a named accountable owner and decision gate.
- Every candidate use case has risk classification, approved data boundaries and an escalation owner.
- Adoption KPIs specify their denominator, collection method and reporting owner.
- A pilot must satisfy security, privacy, model quality and human review before expanding.
- Reported results separate **synthetic examples**, **targets**, **observed metrics** and **realised business value**.
