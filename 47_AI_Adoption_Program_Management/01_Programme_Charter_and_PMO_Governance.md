# 01 — Programme charter and PMO governance

**Case:** Horizon University (fictional). **Programme:** Responsible AI Adoption. **Horizon:** first 90 days; staged expansion subject to gates.

## Business objective
Convert scattered AI pilots into a coordinated portfolio of approved, usable and safe workflows across university administration, teaching support and employee services. Success is **validated usefulness and controlled adoption**, not license count or number of AI prompts.

## Programme scope
**In scope:** use-case discovery/intake, policy reviews, programme governance, vendor evaluation, pilot planning, user literacy, adoption measurement, operational support and benefit verification.
**Out of scope until separately approved:** automated high-stakes student/employment decisions, production integration of sensitive personal data, purchase of new enterprise tools, custom foundational model training, and institution-wide deployment without risk review.

## Governance design
| Forum | Chair / decision owner | Cadence | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive steering | Executive sponsor / COO | Monthly | Budget gates, priority changes, risk appetite |
| AI adoption PMO | Programme manager | Weekly | Delivery risks, milestones, dependency tracking |
| AI assurance review | DPO, Information Security, Legal, AI lead | Before each pilot | Data, model, fairness, procurement and controls approval |
| Workstream stand-up | IT + domain owners + vendors | Weekly | Readiness, unresolved blockers, testing plans |
| Champions feedback session | Change lead + champions | Biweekly | Adoption friction, user guidance improvements |

## Workstreams and outputs
1. **Strategy & intake:** standardised use-case card (business owner, value hypothesis, data sensitivity, alternatives, confidence).
2. **Technology & vendor:** compatibility, procurement assessment, access permissions, secure integration review, acceptance tests.
3. **Risk, security & policy:** privacy impact review, content boundaries, audit trail, approval matrix and incident triggers.
4. **People & change:** communications, role-based AI literacy, manager briefings, champions, help route.
5. **Measurement:** baseline, adoption, quality, time-to-completion, error reports, verified benefits.

## Programme-level artefacts
- Programme charter, RAID log (risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies), governance calendar.
- Milestone / stage-gate log, decision register and approval evidence.
- Vendor work packages, acceptance criteria, UAT sign-off.
- Adoption dashboard with clear numerator, denominator, owner and source.
- Monthly steering pack: progress, value evidence, budget status and decisions required.

## Stage-gate decision
A pilot may start only when its **business owner**, **data classification**, **risk treatment**, **human approval path**, **user guidance**, **success baseline**, **test plan** and **rollback plan** are recorded. No automatic go-live based only on an AI vendor demo.
