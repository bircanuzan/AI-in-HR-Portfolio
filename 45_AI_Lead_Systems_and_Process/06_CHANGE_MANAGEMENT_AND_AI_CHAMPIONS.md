# C — Adoption, Change Management and AI Champions

## Change hypothesis and accountability
Introducing automated checks can create anxiety over exception ownership, effort metrics and customer-facing mistakes. The objective is **human decision quality plus reliable workflows**, not replacing policy owners with a black-box model.

**Sponsor:** Director of AI. **Business owner:** Handover Operations. **Enablement partners:** HR/L&D, IT/Information Security, Finance, Customer Care and Project Delivery. These are fictional proposed roles, not named Azizi stakeholders.

## Change impact assessment
| Audience | AS-IS work | TO-BE change | Adoption risk | Intervention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Handover Coordinators | Email sorting, tracker updates | Review exception queue and case evidence | Fear of lost control/role | Co-design sessions, override with reason, office hours |
| Finance | Ad hoc clearance emails | Structured clearance decision | Escalation overload | Workload forecast, queue allocation, SLA agreement |
| Project Delivery | Readiness status emailed manually | Readiness flag & auditable review | Wrong source of truth | Reference data ownership and exception reconciliation |
| Customer Care | Repeated update calls | Approved templates and transparent status | Incorrect automated promises | Human-approved message library |
| Managers | Inconsistent reporting | Owner-led adoption/benefits dashboard | KPI gaming/misinterpretation | Agreed metric definitions and QA sampling |
| IT/Compliance | Manual incident investigation | Permission and rule audit trails | PII misuse | Access reviews, DLP, retention and tested rollback |

## 30/60/90-day rollout (proposed, no rollout performed)
**Days 1–30 — Prepare:** sponsor alignment, discovery and baseline measurement, process-owner validation, data privacy/IT architecture review, champions nomination, practical demo with synthetic records, SOP draft, learner needs analysis, exit survey.

**Days 31–60 — Pilot:** 1 pilot business unit with limited cases, supervised operations and dual-control approvals, two cohorts trained, daily hypercare, weekly failure/override review, targeted feedback to change rule design. Stop pilot if critical control failure occurs.

**Days 61–90 — Stabilize / decision:** review use-case volume and exception load, calculate actual handling-time delta vs baseline, QA sample, user-adoption pulse survey, refine SOP and role training, submit signed scale/no-scale recommendation.

## AI champions network
- **Composition:** 1 champion per participating handover, Finance, Project Delivery and Customer Care function (proposed).
- **Responsibilities:** peer support, identify adoption barriers, log improvement ideas, flag inaccurate automation claims, deliver monthly office hours and route critical risks to business owners.
- **Operating rhythm:** weekly 30-minute standup during pilot; monthly cross-unit show-and-tell, shared adoption FAQ and change backlog.
- **Measurements:** active trained users, eligible cases processed, correct use of escalation paths, champion response time, issue closure and user confidence.

## HR/L&D enablement plan
| Learning module | Audience | Practice exercise | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1: Workflow and case ownership (30m) | Coordinators/Customer Care | Intake fictional complete/missing pack | Correct route and case note |
| M2: Human approvals and risks (45m) | Finance/Delivery/Handover | Mismatched document and disputed clearance | Appropriate hold, evidence and sign-off |
| M3: Responsible document AI (30m) | All pilot users | Recognize erroneous extraction and uncertainty | No false automation approval |
| M4: Monitoring and continuous improvement (45m) | Managers/champions | Read KPI sample and propose one validated change | Correct baseline, denominator and owner |

**Training artifacts planned:** facilitator guide, quick-reference SOP, learning check, sample ticket, champion FAQ, feedback form and 2-week refresher. Completion is **not** recorded or claimed for any real users.

## Communication sequence
- **T−14 days:** sponsor message: objective, what changes, what remains human, privacy boundaries.
- **T−7 days:** role-based invitations and demonstrations; champions introduce feedback channel.
- **Launch:** approved SOP/version, escalation contacts, hypercare hours and rollback conditions.
- **T+14:** early issues solved, not vanity metrics.
- **T+30/60/90:** sponsor update comparing confirmed baseline, reported exceptions and value reforecast.

## Resistance and corrective action register (example)
| Signal | Likely reason | Corrective action |
|---|---|---|
| Staff bypass the workflow | Slow form or unclear accountability | Observe 5 cases, simplify intake, enforce owner assignment |
| Excessive human-review queue | Overconservative rule/poor data | Segment reasons, improve source data and review only approved thresholds |
| Low trust in extraction | Wrong fields or unclear source | Citation/source preview, confidence gate, sampled QA |
| Low training completion | Sessions conflict with operations | 15-minute microlearning plus champions |
| Perceived surveillance | Misused productivity metrics | Transparent purpose, privacy review, aggregate reporting |

**Change success is not a training attendance percentage alone:** adoption must be accompanied by correct approval behavior, improved service quality and measured business outcomes.
