# Module 3 Storyboard: Everyday Workflow Prompting
**Design artifact for self-paced learning, not evidence of delivered training.**
## Slide-by-slide plan
1. Welcome: objectives and safe-use reminder.
2. Why vague prompts produce inconsistent outputs.
3. Prompt structure: purpose + audience + approved context + constraints + output format.
4. Worked example: transform a generic email request into an actionable prompt.
5. Verification: check every fact against approved material.
6. Sensitive information: redact identifying data and follow policy.
7. Practice: improve a flawed draft using a checklist.
8. Reflect: decide when a human reviewer is required.
## Learner activity (15 minutes)
You must draft an internal announcement of a hypothetical new expense submission process. The only approved facts are: effective 1 November, submitted in the company portal, reviewed by Finance. Write a prompt that prohibits invented policy details. Review sample AI output; mark any statement not backed by these facts.
## Worked sample prompt
"Draft a 120-word internal announcement for employees. Use only these facts: from 1 November expenses are submitted in the company portal and reviewed by Finance. Do not invent deadlines, eligibility rules or approval limits. Flag missing facts as questions. Use clear professional language."
## Answer-check checklist
No added deadlines; correct effective date; correct channel and reviewer; missing details flagged; straightforward and accessible wording.
