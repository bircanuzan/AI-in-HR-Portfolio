# Assessment Bank and Content QA
**Example learning-design assessment, not participant results.**
## Questions and answer key
1. A response cites no policy and invents a reimbursement rate. What should you do? **Do not use it; verify approved policy and report the error.**
2. Is it appropriate to paste employee passport numbers into an unapproved AI chat? **No.**
3. Which prompt is better: "Write a policy" or "Summarize this approved policy excerpt with citations and no new rules"? **The second, when that AI use is authorized.**
4. The assistant provides conflicting instructions from two policy versions. Best action? **Use the approved latest version and ask the owner if unclear.**
5. What is evidence of learning transfer? **Observed appropriate application in workflow, not just attendance.**
## Final task rubric (0–2 per criterion)
Purpose clarity; approved context only; constraints; output format; verification steps; safe escalation. Total /12. Illustrative suggested pass = 10/12 with mandatory safe data handling.
## Development QA checklist
Objectives align to assessment; examples use fictional data; no unapproved product claims; version-controlled source references; captions/alt-text specified; content-owner sign-off required.
