# Enterprise AI Adoption Learning Programme — Design Blueprint
**Original portfolio simulation; not a deployed client programme. Design and development only — NO facilitation or training delivery.**
## Audience and objective
Nontechnical employees and team managers at a hypothetical UAE services organization. Goal: use approved workplace AI tools responsibly for routine information and drafting tasks.
## Needs analysis questions
What tools are licensed? Which tasks are permitted? What data is restricted? What baseline AI literacy exists? Which roles need tailored workflows?
## Four-module journey
| Module | Time | Learning outcomes | Designed materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. AI foundations and limitations | 45 min self-paced | Explain capabilities, hallucinations and verification | storyboard, explainer slides, glossary |
| 2. Responsible AI use | 60 min self-paced | Identify sensitive inputs and approval boundaries | scenarios, decision checklist, knowledge check |
| 3. Everyday workflow prompting | 60 min guided self-study | Draft and critique prompts with context and output constraints | prompt workbook, before/after examples |
| 4. Verify and adopt | 45 min self-paced | Check outputs, document feedback and select safe use cases | verification rubric, application exercise |
## Evaluation design
Pre-check (5 questions); module checks (4 x 3 questions); final scenario-based assessment; post-course workflow confidence survey; optional manager observation after 30 days. These are measurement designs, not claimed outcomes.
## Deliverable package
Slide outlines, storyboards, learner workbook, facilitator-independent instructions, quiz bank, accessibility checks, implementation handover and update register.
## Accessibility and localization
Plain-language copy, readable contrast, keyboard-accessible exported formats, alt text, captions for future media, examples suitable for a multilingual UAE workforce.
