# 01 — HR Change Programme Charter

## Case facts (all hypothetical)
Crescent Services Group employs 650 staff in the UAE across three service locations; 90 line managers and 25 HR team members will be affected to different degrees. It plans to introduce a **vendor-neutral HR employee self-service portal and case-management workflow**.

### Problem statement
The organisation's current email-based HR request process makes response ownership, status and approvals hard to track. The proposed technology creates a channel, but success depends on employees and managers changing how they submit, approve and complete requests.

### Outcomes to be tested — not promises
1. Employees use the approved ESS channel for supported request types.
2. Managers review requests in their assigned workflow rather than email.
3. HR completes cases with auditable ownership, clear policy boundaries and feedback.
4. Duplicate/unclear requests decline without harming accessibility.
5. Employees retain a human support route for exceptions and confidential concerns.

### Scope
**In:** impact assessment, change readiness, sponsor engagement, communications, HR case role mapping, enablement coordination (not course delivery), feedback/concerns, pilot launch support, adoption monitoring and business handover.

**Out:** configuring an HRIS/SSO, migrating historical personal records, writing legal HR policies, approving benefits/leave/employment eligibility, designing autonomous AI decision tools, or certifying cybersecurity/compliance.

### Workstream governance
| Role | Accountability |
|---|---|
| Executive sponsor — CHRO | Visible sponsorship, capacity, policy alignment and go/no-go |
| HR operations process owner | Service definitions, case ownership, service expectations |
| IT product owner | Platform build, access, integration, production incident controls |
| HR change lead | Change plan, impact/readiness, comms, feedback, reporting |
| Managers / HRBPs | Local readiness, escalation and reinforcement |
| Privacy & InfoSec leads | HR data handling, access and security sign-off |
| Implementation vendor | Technical delivery, agreed UAT, defect correction |
| PMO | Integrated milestones, RAID log, dependency management |

### Approach / governance gates
**Gate 0 — Charter:** sponsor and business owner agree scope, decision rights and baseline.
**Gate 1 — Design accepted:** service/process map and impact register signed off.
**Gate 2 — Ready for pilot:** HR policy, privacy, user roles, comms, support, UAT and rollback criteria confirmed.
**Gate 3 — Pilot review:** use/quality/feedback evidence supports expand / amend / pause.
**Gate 4 — Sustainment:** accountable HR operational owners accept support, reporting and reinforcement.

### Deliverables / acceptance
- Signed charter and accountable business sponsor
- Completed impact register and stakeholder analysis
- Readiness baseline with transparent gaps
- Communication schedule and manager briefing pack
- Sponsor/champions programme and resistance log
- Pilot support and adoption dashboard definitions
- Decision log, post-launch action plan and HR handover

### Key uncertainties
Which request categories are safe for ESS? How do non-desk workers access the portal? Which manager approvals are policy-required? What can employees do when accessibility, language or privacy issues arise?

**This charter is a sample programme design, not evidence of an engagement.**
