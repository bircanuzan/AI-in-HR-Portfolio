# 06 — 90-Day HR Change Roadmap, Go-Live & Hypercare

This is an **illustrative** plan for an HR employee self-service pilot; software delivery occurs in a parallel IT/vendor workstream.

| Period | HR change activities | Evidence | Accountable gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–15: Discover | Sponsor interviews, baseline service channels, impact map, staff access mapping, change-saturation review | Stakeholder map, AS-IS process, baseline definitions | CHRO approves scope |
| Days 16–30: Design | Role mapping, stakeholder comms, workforce feedback design, readiness survey template, user-support model | RACI, communication drafts, sample job aids | HR Ops signs process and policy |
| Days 31–45: Validate | IT leads UAT; HR confirms service scenarios; user representatives check accessibility; managers test delegated approval | UAT evidence, access and privacy approvals | IT, HR Ops and Privacy approve pilot |
| Days 46–60: Pilot | Controlled launch in nominated cohort, daily triage, support alternatives, feedback and issue classification | Hypercare log, pilot adoption baseline | Sponsor approves continue/adjust |
| Days 61–75: Reinforce | Fix root causes, target low-readiness teams, review capacity, retest negative cases | Versioned FAQs, issue closure and quality report | HR Ops validates resolved gaps |
| Days 76–90: Sustain | 30/60/90 adoption review, evidence of service quality, handover and scale/hold decision | Executive pack, ownership acceptance and backlog | CHRO scale/hold/stop |

## Integrated dependencies
- **HR service design** must precede employee messaging.
- **Policy and privacy** must be approved before using real personal data in the service portal.
- **Role permissions** must be tested before inviting employees into pilot.
- **Support route** must work before directing staff away from inboxes.
- **Quality guardrails** must pass before widening the population.

## Acceptance scenarios (illustrative)
- Employee submits an eligible employment-letter request, receives a case reference and can check status.
- Manager sees only assigned approval requests and can safely delegate under approved policy.
- Employee without a company laptop can access approved alternate assistance.
- Confidential grievance bypasses general ESS and reaches secure human HR channel.
- Incorrect access, duplicate case and unavailable portal trigger tested recovery routes.

## Hypercare operating model (weeks 1–4 after go-live)
- Daily 15-minute case triage by HR service owner, IT helpdesk and change lead.
- Categorise access, process, communications, privacy, policy and usability issues.
- P1 privacy/access incident: immediately contain and notify InfoSec/Privacy; follow institutional policy.
- P2 workflow-blocking issue: supply approved alternate route, assign owner and update status.
- P3 minor user confusion: revise approved FAQ/job aid, collect examples and retest.
- Weekly evidence review; unresolved high-severity issues are reported to sponsor.

## Go/no-go checklist
**HARD STOP:** privacy control failure, unauthorised role access, no human path for sensitive cases, no case-owner accountability.
**CONDITIONAL HOLD:** insufficient manager readiness, significant workflow defects, inadequate accessibility coverage or no baseline to interpret adoption.
**PROCEED WITH PILOT:** accepted UAT, risk/control sign-offs, staffed support, clear employee message, rollback and measure plan.

No system was configured, tested or launched; UAT cases are proposed only.
