# ⭐ Project 48 — Change Management in HR Program

### A practical, people-first HR transformation case: employee self-service and HR case management

**Bircan Uzan | HumanEvoX | Independent portfolio simulation | Dubai/UAE context**

> **Important:** This case is fictional. No client engagement, HR platform deployment, live employee research, Prosci certification, training delivery, production adoption data, or measured results are claimed. It demonstrates structured documentation and analytical decision-making, not verified implementation experience.

## Executive snapshot
**Organisation:** Crescent Services Group, a fictional multi-site UAE professional-services business with **650 employees, 90 people managers and 25 HR team members** (all invented for the exercise).

**Business change:** Replace HR requests managed through emails, ad hoc spreadsheets and unclear handoffs with a proposed employee self-service (ESS) portal and HR case-management workflow. Platform-neutral: the same change approach could support an approved Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle Fusion HCM or other HR service technology rollout; no configuration is represented.

**Business problem:** Employees do not know where to submit requests; HR has inconsistent request ownership and status visibility; managers are unsure which approvals they own. Installing software alone does not change these behaviours.

**Aim:** Demonstrate how a Change Management lead would plan the **people side of HR technology transformation**: identify impacted teams, secure sponsorship, assess readiness, communicate practical changes, manage resistance, coordinate enablement, and measure sustained behavioural adoption after launch.

## Methodology
A practical **ADKAR-informed** framework (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement), integrated with stakeholder segmentation, a change-impact assessment, sponsor alignment, a readiness gate, process ownership, risk/issue/dependency tracking, user-acceptance-test coordination, behaviour-based adoption metrics and post-go-live reinforcement.

ADKAR is associated with Prosci; this case uses publicly described concepts **without claiming Prosci certification or permission to reproduce proprietary diagnostic instruments**. The custom questions and scorecards in this portfolio are original illustrative templates, not official Prosci tools.

## HRTech and AI's role
- HR technology is the **business change being adopted**, not a system shown as installed. HR and IT retain access, security, employment-policy and service-level ownership.
- AI may help draft **non-confidential communications, FAQs or anonymised themes**; it does not make HR policy decisions or process personal employee information in an unapproved tool. A human verifies every output.
