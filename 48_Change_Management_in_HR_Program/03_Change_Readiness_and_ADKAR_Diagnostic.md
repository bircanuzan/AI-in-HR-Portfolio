# 03 — Change Readiness & ADKAR-Informed Diagnostic

## Why assess readiness?
A system can be technically ready while staff are not ready to use it. This original diagnostic checks sponsorship, role clarity, communications, support access and capability, using **ADKAR-informed** interpretation only. It is **not an official Prosci instrument**, nor proof of certification.

## Original pulse survey (illustrative)
Ask affected groups anonymously on a 1–5 response scale (1 strongly disagree, 5 strongly agree). Include a “not applicable / don't know” option and open feedback.

| Theme | Staff-facing item | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | “I understand which HR requests are moving to the new channel.” | Purpose and scope |
| Desire | “I believe the new route could make my HR requests clearer or easier.” | Motivation and trust |
| Knowledge | “I know where to find approved guidance for submitting a request.” | Instructions and access |
| Ability | “I could complete a common request with available support.” | Actual workflow capability |
| Reinforcement | “My manager and HR team will support this approach after launch.” | Sustained use |
| Policy trust | “I understand how confidential cases and personal information are protected.” | Risk and confidence |

## Operational readiness checklist
| Gate | Evidence | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| Service catalogue | Allowed/ineligible ticket types, approved policy and exceptions | HR Ops |
| Roles & access | Authorised employees/managers mapped and tested | IT/HR |
| Privacy | Approved access, retention, sensitive case handling | Privacy/IT |
| Communications | Audience-specific launch instructions approved | Comms/HR |
| Support | Human escalation, help channels, multilingual/accessibility options | HR Ops |
| Capability | Key task walkthrough and manager approvals verified | HRBPs + users |
| Cutover | UAT sign-off, rollback and defects categorised | IT/PMO |
| Sponsor | Named accountable executive and local champions | CHRO/HRBPs |

## Go/no-go: original logic
**Red:** critical privacy/access control failure, no confidential-case route, unsigned HR policy, or no accountable owner → **no pilot launch**.
**Amber:** minor knowledge/communication gaps with owner, mitigation date and safe workaround → **pilot only if approved**.
**Green:** hard controls passed, key roles capable and incidents/escalation covered → **controlled pilot may proceed**.

Survey averages alone must **never** override hard security, policy or accessibility gates. Report subgroup participation rates; protect anonymity when cohorts are small.

## Hypothetical interpretation
If managers score knowledge 3.8/5 but ability only 2.4/5, the response is not another generic email; arrange a supported practice transaction, verify permissions and retest ability. **The numbers are illustrative, not survey findings.**

## Sampling requirements for a real deployment
Separate employees, managers and HR services; include shifts, locations and access needs; document sample bias; compare pre-pilot and post-pilot results.

**No surveys were administered or scored.**
