# 04 — Communications, Engagement & Culture Plan

## Communication principle
Tell each audience **what changes, what stays human, what they need to do and where they can get help**. Do not promise “AI will replace HR” or market a system as a productivity miracle.

| Timing | Audience | Owner | Medium | Behaviour requested | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 days before pilot | Leaders, HR and IT | CHRO | Sponsor briefing | Confirm purpose and owners | Decision log |
| 21 days before | Managers | HR Change + HRBP | Manager briefing/FAQ | Confirm approval responsibilities | Q&A tracker |
| 14 days before | Pilot employees | HR Communications | Email/Teams/poster | Check eligibility and access | Pulse / support |
| 7 days before | Pilot managers + HR services | HR Ops | Job aid + practice scenario | Demonstrate workflow safely | Practice QA |
| Go-live | Pilot cohort | Business owner | Employee instructions | Submit eligible requests in portal | Helpdesk queue |
| Weeks 1–4 | Pilot cohort | HR Ops | Weekly digest | Correct issues, repeat approved route | Cases + feedback |
| Day 60/90 | Steering committee | HR Change | Evidence report | Approve scale/hold and reinforcement | Decision record |

## Sample email — staff-facing launch (fictional, not sent)
**Subject:** A clearer way to contact HR — pilot starting soon

We're piloting one place for **routine HR requests** so you can track their status and see who is handling them. For pilot employees, common employment-letter requests will be submitted through an approved HR service portal instead of a shared inbox.

**What is NOT changing:** HR advisers remain available for confidential matters, sensitive concerns and complex policy questions. Your manager continues to approve only the requests authorised under existing policy.

**Before the pilot:** check access instructions; tell HR if you cannot use the portal because of device, accessibility or language needs. A human alternative will be available.

**Need help?** Contact the designated HR service support channel (to be specified by the real employer).

## Sample manager briefing — 60-second talking points
- “This is a change in how routine HR requests are recorded, not a change in employee entitlements.”
- “Please use the approval queue for assigned requests; do not approve by informal email.”
- “If an employee is unable to use the portal, refer them to the alternate HR route.”
- “Raise confusing policy cases and access errors early; don't invent workarounds.”

## Culture and engagement
- Invite employee voice through anonymous feedback and short focus groups, if approved.
- Publish a weekly “you said / we changed” update without identifying staff.
- Track trust and perceived service quality, not only portal login counts.
- Respect team shift patterns, local languages and accessibility needs.
- Identify change fatigue if several unrelated initiatives are reaching the same cohort.

### Approval workflow
All messages require HR policy owner approval, information security/privacy review when mentioning data handling, and communications governance where required. AI may assist drafting **generic** materials but should not ingest named employee feedback or confidential HR records.

**The samples have not been issued to actual employees.**
