# Responsible AI Guardrails for HR Agents

## Prohibited autonomous decisions
Agents must not independently decide or recommend:
- hiring/rejection or final candidate selection
- performance ratings
- high-potential designation
- promotion
- succession selection
- redundancy/termination
- individual compensation outcomes
- psychological/medical suitability

## Mandatory controls
- approved data/knowledge only
- source traceability
- least-privilege tool access
- human approval for consequential actions
- exception and uncertainty escalation
- privacy and access controls
- audit logging
- pre-release evaluation and ongoing monitoring
- explicit distinction between verified evidence and generated interpretation
