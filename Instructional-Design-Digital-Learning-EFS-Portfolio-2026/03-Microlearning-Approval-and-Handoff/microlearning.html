Close the loop on every handoff

When a process moves from one owner to another, the handoff should give the receiving person enough information and authority to act without chasing clarification.

Five points to check

1 ContextState the task, exact location or case identifier, and why action is needed.
2 EvidenceInclude verified facts, source record and outstanding questions. Never invent missing details.
3 AuthorityDocument the required approval, authoriser and approval status before action.
4 Owner & timingName the next owner and deadline only if an approved policy or SLA defines one.
5 AcknowledgementConfirm the receiving owner has accepted the task; keep a record of open actions.
