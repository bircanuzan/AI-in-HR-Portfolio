# User Guide and Feedback Process (Sample)
**Hypothetical AI assistant; instructions must be adapted after deployment.**
## Quick start
1. Open the employee knowledge assistant through the company-approved channel.
2. Ask one policy question in natural language, e.g. "Where is the travel reimbursement policy?"
3. Open the cited policy and check the version/effective date.
4. For personal entitlements, payroll issues or uncertainty, use the listed human support channel.
5. Do not enter sensitive personal details unless the company has approved that use.
## Safe usage
The assistant summarizes reference materials; it cannot approve leave, change records or interpret law. Report uncited or contradictory answers.

Weekly review: total submissions, issues by severity, reopened items, policy-source gaps and business-owner decisions. Technical fixes belong to the implementation team.
