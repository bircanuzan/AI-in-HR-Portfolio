## Status definitions
**Green:** accepted deliverables, no unowned blockers, stage-gate evidence on track.  
**Amber:** recoverable at-risk milestone, named mitigation and escalation date.  
**Red:** blocked by material policy/data/control or no credible recovery plan.

## Steering committee decision paper
**Decision ID:** DEC-001  
**Ask:** authorize a limited, *non-production* AI-001 pilot using redacted sample data.  
**Options:** (A) postpone until data platform available, (B) run constrained pilot with no production connectivity, (C) proceed directly to production.  
**Recommendation:** (B), conditional on Architecture, DARB, InfoSec, Risk and business approval.  
**Reason:** tests retrieval usefulness and staff adoption while controlling sensitive data and avoiding an unvalidated production rollout.  
**Exit conditions:** evaluation threshold, documented security/privacy test results, UAT owner sign-off, operating model and roll-back plan.  
**Accountable sponsor:** Merchant Ops sponsor (hypothetical); **approval status:** not obtained.
