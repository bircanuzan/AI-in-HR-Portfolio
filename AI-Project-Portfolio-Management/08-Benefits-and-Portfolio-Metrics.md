# 08 | Benefits Realization and Portfolio KPI Framework
**Purpose:** make value measurable and owned, rather than equating AI usage with business outcomes.

## KPI dictionary
| KPI | Calculation | Baseline/source | Owner | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Handling time | median minutes per reviewed case | Time study, approved sample | Operations | Monthly |
| First-time completeness | Cases with all required fields / cases reviewed | Case records | Quality | Weekly |
| Citation validity | Answers with independently verified support / evaluated answers | QA labeled set | Model QA | Pilot + monthly |
| Abstention correctness | Valid refusals / out-of-scope test cases | Test corpus | AI Safety/QA | Each release |
| Human approval compliance | Cases with named signoff / cases requiring signoff | Audit events | Compliance | Monthly |
| Staff adoption | Trained active users / eligible staff | Learning/SSO logs | Champion | Monthly |
| Delivery on-time | Gates accepted by due date / gates scheduled | Portfolio tracker | Portfolio Lead | Biweekly |
| Blocker age | Median days unresolved for high-severity RAID items | RAID log | Portfolio Lead | Weekly |
| Benefit run rate | Verified units of effort reduced × approved fully loaded unit cost − run costs | Finance-approved data | Finance/Sponsor | Quarterly |
| Safety incidents | Confirmed AI-attributable policy/security incidents | Incident records | Risk | Monthly |

## Benefits logic example
**AI-001 hypothetical pilot:** 10,000 annual cases × 12 minutes baseline × 35% time reduction ÷ 60 = 700 hours.  
At an illustrative AED 200/hour = **AED 140,000 annual gross time-value hypothesis**, **not** confirmed savings, avoidable headcount, or net ROI.  
Net annual value = attributable benefit − platform/model/QA/security/change costs.  
**ROI** = (net annual benefits − one-time implementation cost) / one-time implementation cost, subject to approved assumptions.
**No claim of realized impact until** data ownership, controlled experiment and Finance validation.

## Adoption / experimentation design
Pilot participants and comparable baseline; record differences in case complexity; validate quality parity and privacy. Include quantitative (time/errors) and qualitative (staff feedback/trust) measures. Stop or roll back if control thresholds fail.

## Executive decision triggers
- Red if any critical unmitigated security/data risk, regardless of score.
- Amber if milestones slip > agreed tolerance or benefits baseline unverified.
- Green only when evidence-backed gates, approved controls and owners are current.

## Performance reporting categories
Pipeline health, stage velocity, delivery confidence, risk/issue aging, AI Factory dependency capacity, model effectiveness, user adoption, cost and benefits realization.
