## Business scenario
A fictional, regulated regional payments company is centralizing a scattered pipeline of AI opportunities spanning operations, merchant onboarding and risk. The Head of AI needs a portfolio operating model to convert promising opportunities into governed, measurable deployments while retaining human accountability over high-stakes decisions.

## Simulated initiatives
- **AI-001 | Merchant Onboarding Copilot:** assisted policy/document navigation and case summarization, never automatic KYC approval.
- **AI-002 | Dispute Operations Knowledge Assistant:** retrieves approved policy and drafts explanations, human reviewer controls outcomes.
- **AI-003 | Fraud Alert Triage Insights:** analytical prioritization for review; no autonomous adverse decisions.

## Portfolio operating rhythm
**Weekly:** AI Champion intake / delivery blockers / RAID.  
**Biweekly:** sponsor triage, engineering capacity and interdependency review.  
**Monthly:** Head of AI and Steering Committee decision pack; risk / value / sequencing.  
**Stage gates:** problem qualified → approved prototype → controlled pilot → production release → measured benefits.

## Methods and boundaries
Weighted multi-criteria prioritization; BRD & user journey; option comparison; RACI/RAID; DARB readiness; privacy-by-design; human-in-the-loop; UAT traceability; preproduction performance/safety testing; operational acceptance; KPI baselines; change control.
