# 06 | Assessor Calibration, Data Integration, QA and Fairness

## Practice design
Two fictional assessors independently rated **12 evidence items** on the same 1–5 anchored scale. The records intentionally include two rating differences to demonstrate a proper evidence discussion.

| Evidence item | Assessor A | Assessor B | Integrated rating | Resolution |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| E06 — invited quiet participant, negotiated but missed ownership | 4 | 3 | **3** | Positive inclusion, but incomplete agreed ownership |
| E12 — constructive group feedback, no measurable follow-up | 3 | 4 | **3** | No observable measurement/check-in, so anchored at 3 |

Other ten items show matching A/B ratings and were still reviewed for evidence.

## Calibration outcome (fictional)
- Exact agreement: **10/12 = 83.3%**.
- Chance-adjusted agreement (Cohen's kappa): **about 0.692** for this small, balanced fictional dataset.
- All 12 final ratings have a written rationale and the final competency-level results.
- There is **no official reliability certification, validation study or population benchmark** from this exercise.

## Decision and evidence requirements
1. Every evidence item must exist and map to one of the agreed competencies/exercises.
2. Two independent records (A and B) must be present for each item.
3. Rater notes must record behavior; not labels like "confident personality" or "poor cultural fit".
4. Disagreements are resolved against pre-agreed behavioral anchors, not average-by-default.
5. Final integration is documented; unresolved cases must remain flagged rather than silently filled.
6. Report scores with uncertainty, sources, context and human review.
7. No automatic hire/reject recommendation based on a single case.

## Bias and accessibility controls
Assessors receive frame-of-reference training, blind independent recording where feasible and calibration before integration. Accommodations and candidate rights are documented. Observe work-relevant behavior rather than accent, disability, communication style preferences, nationality or other irrelevant factors. Avoid interpreting language fluency as competency unless necessary and appropriately assessed.

## Data protection and independence
Synthetic IDs are used throughout. Real assessment information needs participant notices, access purpose, retention, lawful basis, encrypted approved storage and defined recipients. No public GitHub upload of candidate data. Avoid a direct manager rating their subordinate when conflicts of interest may affect a selection process.

## Programme QA checklist
- Job analysis and exercise relevance defined
- Competency descriptions and exercise mapping approved
- Assessor training/qualification checked
- Candidate briefing and accommodations handled
- Observation notes written independently
- Ratings have evidentiary support
- Calibration decisions documented
- Report reviewed for fairness and accuracy
- Feedback provider authorized
- Coaching and selection purposes kept separate
- Results access, retention and disposal approved
