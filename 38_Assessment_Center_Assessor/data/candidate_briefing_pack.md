# Candidate briefing pack — entirely fictional

**Candidate:** Candidate A (synthetic identifier, not a real person)  
**Role:** People Operations Manager  
**Purpose:** Development-oriented demonstration, not employment selection  
**Exercises:** 30-min in-basket; 20-min role-play; 25-min group discussion  
**Duration:** 75 minutes of exercises, plus separate briefing and candidate debrief

## Participant instructions
You will receive the same fictional materials and timing as other participants. Complete each exercise as you ordinarily would. You may ask for clarification, state assumptions, identify data limitations and explain decision trade-offs. Assessment observations concern **job-relevant behavior during the exercises**, not personal identity or untested personality attributes.

### In-basket material
A fictional HR service center reports 24 delayed cases; a category report marks 10 as payroll, while the quality audit says 3 require reclassification as policy requests. Two payroll actions require approved signatures. Respond to HR leadership with priorities, owner assignments, evidence needs and risk controls.

### Role-play material
A skeptical Finance Manager believes the proposed HR automation lacks sufficient reconciliation checks. The manager will describe incomplete data and concern over rework if the participant asks relevant diagnostic questions. Propose a controlled pilot or another suitable recommendation.

### Group simulation material
A draft initiative assumes 25% faster handling with no independent baseline. Stakeholders disagree on automation controls. Seek facts, develop a cross-functional plan, assign decision rights and include all participants.

## Consent, fairness and confidentiality
In a real assessment, the organization would provide actual privacy and accessibility notices, intended result use, accommodations and an authorized assessor process. This fictional file does not collect or store personal data and is not a formal consent form.
