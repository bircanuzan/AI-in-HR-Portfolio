# 04 | Candidate Feedback and Debrief — 30-Minute Facilitation Guide

**For a fictional Candidate A; English-language template.** A real assessor must be properly trained and authorized to give project-specific assessment feedback.

## Suggested agenda
| Minutes | Facilitator action | Candidate experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Explain purpose, confidentiality, sources, assessment limits | Candidate confirms understanding and expectations |
| 5–10 | Invite self-reflection: what felt effective/challenging? | Candidate describes their own exercise experience |
| 10–16 | Explore two strengths with specific evidence | Candidate hears behavior, not labels or personality judgements |
| 16–23 | Discuss decision-making control gap with examples | Candidate can challenge, clarify and offer missing context |
| 23–27 | Co-create practical development actions and support | Candidate chooses feasible goal |
| 27–30 | Summarize, ask for feedback, agree next check-in | Candidate knows next steps and reporting boundaries |

## Example conversation
**Opening:** "This conversation focuses on behaviors observed in the practice exercises. It isn't a personality judgement or a final statement about your suitability for every role."

**Evidence-based strength:** "In the group exercise, you questioned whether the 25% improvement assumption had been proven and asked for baseline data. That was useful analytical judgment in the scenario."

**Invite perspective:** "What helped you decide to challenge that assumption? Would you approach it the same way in a real project?"

**Development example:** "During the role-play, the pilot had a clear intent, but no rollback test was specified. What would you add to make that decision safer?"

**Coach, don't prescribe:** "What is one specific change you could practise in the next month, and who could give you feedback?"

**Close:** "Let's agree a specific action and review date, and keep this report available only to the people authorized to access it."

## Feedback safeguards
- Distinguish observed behavior from a person's identity or presumed motivation.
- Give specific examples, invite correction and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Do not imply the assessment predicts performance with certainty.
- Avoid sharing other participants' results.
- Agree who will receive results and how long they will be retained.
- Use appropriately trained and authorized feedback providers; provide communication support/accommodations where needed.
- If Arabic reporting is requested, use an appropriately qualified professional; do not present machine translation as a certified assessor's report.
