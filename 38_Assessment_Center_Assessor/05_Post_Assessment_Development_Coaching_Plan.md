## 30/60/90-day reinforcement
- **30 days:** role-play a business case; make one explicit approval gate and one fallback.
- **60 days:** apply to three scenarios; collect supervisor/peer evidence and review completeness.
- **90 days:** evaluate whether follow-up actions, stakeholder accountability and risk escalation improved and identify next area of growth.

## Learning interventions
- Decision memo template: opportunity, data confidence, options, risks, owner, approval, rollback and measurement.
- Meeting closeout checklist: named owner, due date, dependency, agreement and check-in.
- Coaching practice: ask for a person's interpretation, explore alternatives, co-create one measurable next step.
