# 07 | Assessor Credential and Language Transparency

## The role's stated requirements (per supplied vacancy description)
- Recognized assessor certification, such as BPS or accepted equivalent.
- Assessment-centre practice conducting standardized observations, ratings, reports and feedback.
- English **and Arabic** reporting.

## What this portfolio demonstrates
- An original example of a competency framework, three exercise structures and a behavioral rating system.
- A full illustrative evidence log, two-assessor calibration, written report, feedback discussion and developmental coaching plan.
- **English-language written assessment documentation.**
- A reproducible basic scoring and QA demonstration using Python.

## What it cannot establish
- Successful BPS certification, current assessor qualification or equivalence.
- Authorized independent observation/rating of real candidates under an accredited assessment center.
- Arabic oral or written reporting ability.
- Official BPS endorsement of the repository or any commercial client assignment.

## Coaching credentials are different
An **ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC)** credential, where independently verifiable, is relevant to development coaching and structured debriefing but **is not automatically an assessor certification or BPS equivalence**. Assess International decides what other credentials it accepts.

## Candidate-eligibility checklist before applying
1. Ask whether the company accepts relevant assessor training/experience or requires specific BPS-approved training.
2. Confirm the Arabic writing/reporting requirement and whether bilingual-assessor team arrangements are permitted.
3. Provide only truthful evidence of independently delivered assessment-center work, if any.
4. Request information on its assessor standard, competency framework, reporting templates, confidentiality and calibration expectations.
5. If proceeding, offer an **English-language illustrative report** as portfolio material rather than claiming Arabic skills or certification.
