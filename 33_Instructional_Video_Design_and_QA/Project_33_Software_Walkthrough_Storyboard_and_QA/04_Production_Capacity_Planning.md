# Production capacity and commercial planning — illustrative

Volume commitments must follow a proven pilot, named editing capacity and measured effort.

## Formulae
- Monthly finished-video hours required = total planned finished hours / number of months.
- Total work hours = finished-video hours × measured production-effort multiplier.
- Monthly available production work hours = staffed editors × productive hours per editor.
- Capacity gap = monthly available production work hours − required production work hours.
- Price estimate = finished-video hours × agreed price per delivered hour, plus agreed revision/complexity adjustments.

## Hypothetical workload
For a 600-hour / 12-month programme, average finished output = 50 hours monthly. This does **not** imply 50 working hours; editing typically requires more effort and the actual multiplier must be measured from a pilot.
