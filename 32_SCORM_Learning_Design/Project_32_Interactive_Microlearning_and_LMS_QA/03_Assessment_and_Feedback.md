# Illustrative assessment and item feedback

**Mode:** formative quiz; five items. **Scoring proposal:** 1 point each; pilot pass threshold 4/5 pending business approval.

1. **What is the best first response to an unclear product request?**  
   A. Offer a popular product. **B. Ask how and where it will be used.** C. Promise a feature not yet verified.  
   Feedback: Understand the problem before suggesting a solution.

2. **What is the most reliable basis for a product claim?**  
   A. Memory alone. **B. Current approved reference material.** C. Any internet comment.  
   Feedback: Verify factual claims against approved material.

3. **A customer provides a new constraint late in the conversation. What next?**  
   **A. Revisit the recommendation.** B. Ignore it. C. Repeat the initial answer.  
   Feedback: Updated requirements can change the best next step.

4. **You cannot verify a technical detail. What should you do?**  
   A. Guess. B. Invent a guarantee. **C. Escalate to an approved expert/source.**  
   Feedback: Accuracy and transparency are more important than speed.

5. **Which ending demonstrates a needs-led conversation?**  
   A. "This option suits everyone." **B. "Based on your stated needs, the next step is to compare approved options."** C. "No questions are necessary."  
   Feedback: Link advice to evidence and explain the next action.

## Reporting design
Potential data fields: attempt status, completion, score and duration **only where the agreed SCORM version and LMS support them**. No actual learner attempts or test data exist.
