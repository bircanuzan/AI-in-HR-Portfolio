# Learning blueprint — needs-led customer conversations

**Fictional programme:** Customer Discovery Essentials  
**Target:** new retail adviser; 12 minutes; mobile-friendly design intent.  
**Prerequisite:** access to approved, current product information supplied by the content owner.

## Performance objectives
At the end of the module, the learner should be able to:
1. Ask at least three relevant discovery questions before recommending a product.
2. Separate verified product facts from assumptions.
3. Explain an appropriate next step using the customer's stated needs.
4. Recognize when a specialist or supervisor consultation is required.

## Structure and timing

| Stage | Minutes | Learning action | Observable result |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hook | 1 | See a customer question and choose first response | Notices premature assumption |
| Discover | 3 | Practice questions about context, use and constraints | Selects three relevant questions |
| Explain | 3 | Read approved information and spot unsupported claims | Chooses approved source only |
| Practice | 3 | Complete branching customer scenario | Gives needs-led advice |
| Check | 2 | Complete five-item knowledge test | Receives item feedback |

## Accessibility, quality and control
Captions and transcripts for audio; keyboard and focus states for all interactions; no color-only correct/incorrect indicators; source-approval and revision tracking. Specific WCAG target must be agreed by stakeholders.

## Development status
Blueprint and content structure are complete as a written sample. Technical authoring, SCORM packaging and LMS compatibility are **not** implemented.
