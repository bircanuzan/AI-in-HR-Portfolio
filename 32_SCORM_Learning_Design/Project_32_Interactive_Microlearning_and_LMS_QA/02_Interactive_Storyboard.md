# Interactive storyboard — sample screens

| Scene | Timing | On-screen copy | Learner interaction | Feedback and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Hook | 00:00–01:00 | "A customer asks: Which option is best for me?" | Choose: recommend immediately / ask about intended use | Feedback: discover use before proposing a solution |
| 02 Discover | 01:00–04:00 | "Learn the setting, constraints and preferences" | Select three questions from a mixed list | Target questions explained; irrelevant questions flagged |
| 03 Explain | 04:00–07:00 | "Use only the approved product card" | Identify a statement unsupported by the card | Flag unsupported product claim; refer to content owner |
| 04 Practice | 07:00–10:00 | Fictional customer has specific use and maintenance constraints | Branch through discovery, explanation and next step | Reasoned feedback for each branch |
| 05 Verify | 10:00–12:00 | Five-question check and completion summary | Answer items, review feedback | Score/completion approach to be agreed for build |

## Branch example: Scene 04

**Customer:** "I need a product for a frequently used area, but I haven't settled on the specifications."

- **Option A:** Recommend the most expensive item without questions. *Feedback:* Premature and unsupported.
- **Option B:** Ask about frequency of use, conditions, preferences and confirmed requirements. *Feedback:* Appropriate discovery.
- **Option C:** Promise a performance feature that is not shown in the approved materials. *Feedback:* Do not make unsupported claims.

**Design notes for production:** screen-reader labels, logical focus order, accessible alternative to drag interactions, explanation on incorrect attempts, branded assets and approved facts supplied later. No real manufacturer product details are represented.
