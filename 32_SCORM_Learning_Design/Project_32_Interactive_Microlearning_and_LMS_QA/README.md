# Project 32 — Interactive Microlearning and LMS QA

## Problem
Retail advisers must make recommendations based on customer needs rather than jumping to unverified product claims.

## Learning outcome
After a 12-minute module, a learner can conduct a short needs-discovery conversation, select a customer-appropriate next step and explain why without inventing product information.

## Design model
- Audience: fictional new retail advisers.
- Duration: 12 minutes.
- Modality: self-paced interactive module, keyboard-accessible design intent.
- Assessment: scenario choices plus a five-question knowledge check; threshold is illustrative and must be approved.
- Production boundary: storyboard and QA design **only**; no tested SCORM export or real LMS results.
