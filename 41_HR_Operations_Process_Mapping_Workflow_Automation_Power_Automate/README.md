# Improving HR operations through process mapping, workflow automation and practical AI adoption (Microsoft Power Automate)

**Bircan Uzan | HumanEvoX | Project 41**

> Independent portfolio case study. Fictional organisation and illustrative assumptions. This repository contains a business-side solution design, not an exported or deployed Power Automate flow. No live test results or client outcomes are claimed.

## The business problem
At a fictional 250-person professional-services company, employee requests for standard employment-confirmation letters arrive by email. HR checks details, chases authorisation, updates a spreadsheet and sends status emails. Ownership and approval status are difficult to see.

## The project
Redesign this one service around structured intake, a tracked request, human approval and a visible exception queue. Apply topics from Microsoft Learn's [Demonstrate the capabilities of Microsoft Power Automate](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/demonstrate-capabilities-microsoft-power-automate/).

## Skills demonstrated
HR service improvement · process mapping · business requirements · workflow design · approval governance · Power Automate concepts · Microsoft 365 integration design · practical Copilot adoption · UAT planning · operational measurement.

**AI role:** propose flow structures and explain logic during development; a human checks the design. The proposed employee-service workflow uses explicit rules. AI does not authorise letters or determine employee entitlements.
