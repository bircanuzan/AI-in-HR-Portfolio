# Board Risk Committee — simulated decision brief
**Period:** SampleCo inception baseline, 29 October 2026. **Label:** mock executive pack. No real enterprise data, results or approvals.

## 1. Strategic question
How can the organisation scale AI in customer and employee workflows **without losing accountability, privacy, fairness, resilience or auditability**?

## 2. Decision requested
Approve **the design and resourcing of an AI Governance Office and controlled sandbox programme only**. Do **not** approve production deployment of any of the four listed systems.

## 3. Baseline picture (fictional)
- 4 candidate systems inventoried; 2 internal T3 (high) and 2 T2 (moderate).
- 14 risk scenarios identified, most controls lack real technical assurance.
- 0 independent technical model validations performed; 0 verified privacy sign-offs; 0 audited deployment decisions.
- These are demonstration design counts, not organisation-wide maturity KPIs.

## 4. Principle risks
| Priority | Risk | Proposed executive treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Candidate ranking discriminates or fabricates credentials | No autonomous rejection, independent testing and human appeal |
| 2 | Customer/employee personal data exposed to model supplier | DPIA, processor terms, restricted flows, regional transfer review |
| 3 | Agent executes tool actions without authority | Least privilege, tool allowlist, mandatory human approval and kill switch |
| 4 | Model/version changes invalidate assurance | Continuous inventory, version pinning, regression and monitoring |
| 5 | Governance paperwork mistaken for actual controls | Independent reviewer verifies technical logs and raw evaluations |

## 5. Target operating model and accountabilities
Business owner accountable; Engineering builds/test; Data Steward ensures data quality and lineage; DPO reviews privacy; CISO reviews security; Responsible AI Office owns policies/risk process; independent QA challenges; Committee signs high-risk release; Internal Audit periodically verifies governance effectiveness.

## 6. 30/60/90-day illustrative roadmap
- **Days 1–30:** define charter, committee, criteria and inventory; interview stakeholders; select and document one synthetic sandbox; legal applicability screening.
- **Days 31–60:** implement intake and evidence workflow in a trial tool; establish vendor reviews; design lawful TEVV and human override; train accountable owners.
- **Days 61–90:** independently validate a *real* low-risk sandbox only if enterprise permissions and tested evidence exist; rehearse incident response; report governance metrics and next-phase investment decision.

## 7. Approval and critical dependencies
Proposed budget and ownership require sponsor approval; any live pilot needs security, privacy and independent QA evidence. Do not present this README or the deterministic gate output as production certification.

## 8. Executive challenge questions
Who is accountable for adverse AI decisions? Can affected people challenge them? Which supplier can access sensitive inputs? What happens when a foundation model or agent's permissions change? How quickly can the organisation isolate harmful output or suspend an agent? What evidence would independent auditors actually see?
