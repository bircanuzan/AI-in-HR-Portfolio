# Consolidated role capability roadmap
This is a **capability-building plan**, not a record of earned qualifications or tools already administered.

## Capabilities that transfer from HR transformation / delivery
- Organisation design, stakeholder engagement and governance forums.
- Learning, training, adoption and responsible change management.
- Talent assessment logic, competency frameworks and human review.
- Business-process mapping, workflow UAT and executive reporting.
- Risk, action tracking and benefit measurement.

## AI governance competence to demonstrate next
| Skill | Target deliverable | Proposed learning tools or sources | Evidence standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 42001 AI management systems | Organisational AI policy, AIMS scope, accountability and improvement cycle | ISO overview and approved training | Correct annotated charter plus gap assessment, not certification |
| NIST AI RMF and ISO 23894 | Risks, impacts, treatment and residual-risk approvals | NIST AI RMF Playbook, ISO guidance | Human-reviewed impact and control register |
| Regulatory scoping and privacy | Jurisdiction assessment, DPIA and data processing record | UAE PDPL official reference; GDPR/EU AI Act official texts; qualified counsel | Lawful-basis memo reviewed by expert, not self-certified |
| Responsible AI fairness and XAI | Fairness test protocol, representative dataset, limits and explanation | scikit-learn, Fairlearn, SHAP | Verified actual dataset and reproducible output; **not present** |
| GenAI safety and security | Adversarial prompt test set, grounding, misuse, leakage | NIST GenAI Profile; OWASP LLM guidance; Azure safety documentation | Real tested model traces and independent reviewer; **not present** |
| Agentic control plane | Tool permissions, approval tokens, monitoring | Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Automate, Azure AI Foundry | Tenant-backed permission and action traces; **not present** |
| Model governance / lifecycle | Model cards, versioning, drift and red-team testing | MLflow, Evidently, responsible ML guidance | Automated data/model monitoring logs; **not present** |
| Enterprise data governance | Data catalog, stewardship and lineage | Microsoft Purview, Fabric; Palantir Foundry concepts | Actual platform screenshot/config/export with permission; **not present** |
| Governance workflow automation | Intake -> assessment -> review -> go/no-go | ServiceNow GRC/SPM or Microsoft Power Platform | Working application with UAT evidence; **not present** |
| Audit and reporting | Control testing, evidence manifests, committee KPIs | Python, Power BI, SQL, GRC workflow tooling | Reproducible metrics from actual systems; **not present** |

## Focused sequential learning recommendation (one course at a time)
**First:** NIST AI RMF 1.0 Core + Playbook. Understand GOVERN/MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE and complete one use-case impact assessment. **Then** study ISO/IEC 42001 management-system requirements and translate policy into roles and evidence. **Then** perform authorised hands-on testing against a real sandbox, documenting reproducible model responses and independent review.

Do not claim possession of CIPP/E, CIPM, ISO 42001 Lead Implementer, ML engineering, formal AI ethics certification or a live enterprise deployment without proof.

## Practical job-title positioning
Most directly transferable: AI Governance Analyst / Specialist; Responsible AI Programme Manager; AI Governance Operations Manager; AI Risk & Controls Manager (depending on technical requirements). Harder specialist gaps: Data Protection Officer, AI Model Validation Manager, Head of AI Ethics, technical ML QA lead. Portfolio deliverables are transferable evidence of project design, not a substitute for years of dedicated experience.
