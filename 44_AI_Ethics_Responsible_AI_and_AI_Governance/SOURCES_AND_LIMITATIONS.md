## Limits and intellectual integrity
- Job descriptions summarised in original language, not copied wholesale; all source rights belong to their publishers.
- Standards cited from free public overviews; this project **does not reproduce paywalled ISO standards**.
- A reference to ISO, NIST, GDPR, DIFC or EU AI Act is not a claim of certification or automatic legal applicability.
- Synthetic organisational example, no real employee/candidate/claims information.
- No production ML training, live LLM inference, agent deployment, platform integration, model audit, or tested fairness measurements.
- A local rule-based approval script can validate only submitted control flags; it cannot validate whether underlying evidence is authentic.
- This project demonstrates deliberate design, risk literacy, business/technical interface, and the ability to create reproducible **local governance-policy checks**, not verified years of AI engineering practice.
