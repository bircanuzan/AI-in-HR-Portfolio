# 01 — Psychometric Assessment & Talent Evaluation: Project Charter

## Fictional business brief
A UAE group requests an analytical review of three talent scenarios:
- **Hiring** four Senior HR Analyst applicants (C001–C004).
- **Promotion** four People Operations Manager candidates (C005–C008).
- **Succession/development** four potential HR Transformation Leads (C009–C012).

**Business questions:** Which evidence supports role-relevant capability? What strengths or development needs merit investigation? Where do assessment conditions or score interpretation make recommendations unreliable? What additional job-based evidence should HR collect before a human decision?

## Consultant's proposed delivery model
| Step | Key input | Work product | Required control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Job criteria, purpose, informed participant notice | Assessment blueprint | Role relevance approved by HR |
| Intake | Authorized vendor's test results, score manual, conditions | Evidence register | Qualified user confirms test permissions and interpretation scope |
| Validation | Data, scores, norms and administration metadata | Data-quality/limitation log | Stop interpretation where provenance, suitability or validation is missing |
| Analysis | Approved results plus independent work evidence | Draft individual findings | No unsupported trait or suitability inference |
| Reporting | Case reports and grouped data | HR insights and development actions | Qualified reviewer sign-off |
| Feedback | Approved reports | Individual and group debriefs | Privacy and participant access controls |
| Decision | Holistic evidence | Recommendations for HR's consideration | Human decision maker owns outcomes |

## Explicitly excluded work
Vocational training, candidate role-play facilitation, observed behavioural assessment centres, clinical diagnosis, test construction, unsupervised administration of restricted proprietary instruments, automated rejection/approval.

## Proposed acceptance measures (not achieved outcomes)
- Every report states construct, purpose, instrument evidence, uncertainty and review status.
- No unvalidated cut-offs or score-only employment decisions.
- All test-user permissions and participant notices recorded before use.
- No identifiable employee or candidate information published or reused.
