# 09 — Psychometric Governance, Privacy, Fairness & QA

## Live engagement guardrails
**Professional competence:** A reporting analyst may synthesize authorized inputs, but should not claim independent rights to administer, interpret or release restricted psychological test outputs without appropriate training, publisher access and client authority. The BPS Test User Occupational qualification is a common competence pathway, **not a universal UAE government licence**.

**Data safeguards (UAE):** Verify applicable UAE personal-data-protection requirements, lawful basis, purposes, access controls, minimization, retention/deletion, processor roles and cross-border hosting arrangements. Health/clinical assessment requires separate professional licensing, beyond this corporate-occupational case.

**Test quality:** Investigate reliability, validity, relevant normative populations, confidence/measurement error, language and translation, disability accommodations, remote versus supervised onsite equivalence, and the consequences of selection bias.

**Fairness:** Check adverse impacts using adequate, appropriately authorized data and methodology. This synthetic n=12 dataset **cannot** be used to claim the existence or absence of bias.

**AI boundary:** Generative AI may assist with document formatting or redacted summaries only when allowed by contract and privacy rules; it must not invent score interpretations, diagnose personality or make employment recommendations on its own.

## Pre-release QA checklist
- [ ] Clear assessment purpose and valid job analysis
- [ ] Qualified test-user sign-off for interpretation
- [ ] Publisher permission, test version, manuals and score provenance
- [ ] Conditions, language, accommodations, relevant norms and caveats checked
- [ ] No unjustified score thresholds or personality-to-performance leaps
- [ ] Interview and work-sample evidence separately documented
- [ ] Candidate identity and privacy protected
- [ ] Human decision owner identified
- [ ] Reviewer approval, report date and version recorded

## Technical validation warning
This demonstration contains **no validated psychometric instruments or norms** and no legitimate inference about candidate personality, intelligence, potential, or fitness. It is a **portfolio demonstration of document structure, analytical caution and decision governance**.
