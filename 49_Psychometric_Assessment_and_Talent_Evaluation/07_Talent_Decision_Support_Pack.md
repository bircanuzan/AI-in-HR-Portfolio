# 07 — Hiring, Promotion, Succession & Development: HR Decision Pack

## Decision principles
Actual employment decisions require valid job-related evidence, qualified psychometric interpretation where restricted instruments are used, procedural fairness and accountable human approval.

| Scenario | Evidence to integrate | Illustrative decision-support output | Action owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hiring** | Validated cognitive test output, structured interview, relevant analyst work sample | Describe strengths/hypotheses and open evidence questions; do **not** issue score-only rankings | HR + hiring manager |
| **Promotion** | Validated occupational assessment if authorized, operational KPIs, leadership evidence, role expectations | Readiness **questions**, development and exposure plan; no personality-driven promotion verdict | Talent committee |
| **Succession** | Performance record, delivery outcomes, role-fit evidence, authorized assessment output | Succession evidence map, experience gaps, follow-up timing; no predicted successor from questionnaire | CHRO + succession owner |
| **Leadership development** | Role requirements, employee goals, feedback, relevant strengths | Development suggestions tied to observable work results, follow-up and coaching | Manager + employee |

## Example next-step plan for fictional C003
1. Provide an accessible HR data case exercise with documented scoring anchors.
2. Invite the participant to discuss data interpretation methods and reporting decisions.
3. If genuine restricted psychometric data are later used, involve an appropriately qualified and publisher-authorized test user.
4. Have HR record the combined evidence and reasons for its final decision.

## Caution on risk labels
A style preference is **not** a “risk flag” by itself. Risks should refer to **process/data quality** (e.g., missing norms, unsuitable language, incomplete consent, out-of-scope interpretation) or to observable, corroborated job-related performance evidence.

## Deliverable quality gate
Report: [ ] job relevance checked [ ] score interpretation authorized [ ] limitations stated [ ] corroboration identified [ ] review completed [ ] human decision recorded.
