# 02 — Job Analysis, Constructs and Evidence Blueprint

| Talent decision | Role demands | Relevant assessment data (illustrative only) | Corroboration | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Senior HR Analyst hiring | Numerical reasoning, reporting and written interpretation | Numerical, verbal and abstract fictional ability indices | Analytical work sample, structured interview, role-specific performance task | Automatic rejection or norm claims based on arbitrary indices |
| People Operations Manager promotion | Judgment, coordination, HR operations, conflict escalation | Cognitive-context indices; work-style discussion prompts | Past performance and documented leadership outcomes | Equating style preferences with promotion readiness |
| HR Transformation Lead succession | Strategy, synthesis, change delivery and influence | Reasoning patterns and work-style discussion prompts | Transformation deliverables, stakeholder evidence, case review | Treating one questionnaire as a forecast of leadership potential |

## Qualified test-user due diligence in live projects
**Purpose & job analysis:** Is the instrument suitable for the purpose and role?  
**Instrument evidence:** Which reliability/validity studies, relevant norms, confidence intervals and test versions apply?  
**Access:** Is the interpreter trained and publisher-authorized for the selected instrument?  
**Conditions:** Were online and onsite administrations equivalent, accessible, appropriately supervised and ethically handled?  
**Fairness:** Are relevant language, cultural or demographic effects addressed using sufficient evidence?  
**Interpretation:** Are claims constrained by the manuals, uncertainty and appropriate role-based context?  
**Human governance:** Who makes the final employment decision and handles reconsideration requests?

**Design principle:** Psychometric results inform structured human decision-making; they do not substitute for relevant, corroborating evidence.
