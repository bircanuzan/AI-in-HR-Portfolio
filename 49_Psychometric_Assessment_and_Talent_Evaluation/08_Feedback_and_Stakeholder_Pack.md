# 08 — Feedback & Stakeholder Communication Pack

## For HR leadership — 15-minute virtual or onsite review
**Opening:** “The purpose of this analysis is to provide disciplined insight for a hiring, promotion or development conversation. The sample data demonstrate reporting methods and **do not constitute valid psychometric evidence**.”

**Agenda**
1. Decision context and business outcome sought.
2. Summary of assessment conditions and qualified-interpreter checks.
3. Job-relevant evidence and uncertainty.
4. Findings requiring further corroboration.
5. Development priorities and operational next steps.
6. Ownership, review, accessibility, retention and participant feedback.

**Suggested HR briefing on fictional C003:** “The independently scored hypothetical work sample and interview are promising contextual inputs. The arbitrary ability-related indices are not normed test results and cannot establish suitability. Request a role-specific analytical work sample before moving to a human decision.”

## Candidate feedback — respectful, non-diagnostic
“This information is one piece of evidence, not a definition of your ability or potential. We will consider role-specific tasks, interview evidence, the suitability of the test and any assessment conditions. We can explain what the assessment was designed to measure and offer appropriate feedback.”

## Remote feedback controls
Private meeting, participant identity confirmation, secure approved sharing, no public recording, accessible presentation and documented follow-up.

## Onsite feedback controls
Private room, confidential printed report handling, approved attendees only, controlled collection/shredding of paper materials and no unauthorized photography.

## Escalation examples
- Candidate disputes accessibility or language suitability → suspend interpretation and refer to assessment owner.
- Test publisher authorization unclear → no restricted interpretation or report release.
- Report proposes automatic rejection → escalate to HR/legal and qualified assessor.
