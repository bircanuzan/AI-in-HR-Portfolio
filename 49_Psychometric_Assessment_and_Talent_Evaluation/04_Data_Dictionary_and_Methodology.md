# 04 — Data Dictionary and Methodology

## Important methodological disclaimer
Every number in this project is **invented**. Indices on an arbitrary 0–100 demonstration scale are **not actual psychometric results, validated scores, percentiles, IQ scores, normed cut-offs or measures of a real person**.

| Field | Demonstration meaning | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| case_id | Anonymous fictional record | Not personally identifiable |
| purpose / role | Business context | Three cohorts cannot be pooled as one comparable normative population |
| administration | Remote / Onsite | Not evidence of administration equivalence |
| numerical_index, verbal_index, abstract_index | Fictional cognitive-related descriptors | Not a validated cognitive assessment or normed test |
| planning_style, collaboration_style, adaptability_style | Fictional preference-style descriptors | Not evidence of competence, mental health or suitability |
| structured_interview_index | Independent hypothetical interview rating | Not a psychometric score |
| work_sample_index | Independent hypothetical task rating | Not a psychometric score |

## Descriptive analysis
The demo script checks the 12 records and computes the arithmetic mean of selected fields by cohort.

A **toy calculation only**: `0.4 × numerical_index + 0.35 × verbal_index + 0.25 × abstract_index`. The formula has **no validation evidence**. It is not used for candidate ranking, hiring decisions, norms or individual diagnosis.

**Group mean** = sum of fictional numerical values / number of fictional records in the group. Group sizes are 4; these are **descriptive values only**, with no significance test, representativeness claim, comparative fairness conclusion or clinical use.

## Report validation gates
1. Establish test source, version, date, score metric and manual.
2. Verify qualified interpreter, permissions and participant basis for processing.
3. Verify administration conditions, translation, accommodations and relevant norms.
4. Detect missing, duplicated or invalid records.
5. Identify what data cannot support an inference.
6. Request non-test evidence to corroborate key hypotheses.
7. Document reviewer, sign-off, report purpose, access and retention.
