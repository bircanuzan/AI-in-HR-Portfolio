# 06 — Group Talent Assessment Insights (Illustrative)

Each cohort contains **four synthetic records**. Reported means are rounded to 1 decimal place. These are arbitrary descriptive values, not group norms or validated performance differences.

| Fictional group | n | Numerical | Verbal | Abstract | Structured interview | Work sample |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hiring — Senior HR Analyst | 4 | 78.5 | 73.8 | 75.0 | 79.5 | 79.0 |
| Promotion — People Operations Manager | 4 | 77.0 | 80.0 | 79.0 | 84.3 | 84.0 |
| Succession — HR Transformation Lead | 4 | 79.3 | 79.3 | 77.8 | 82.3 | 85.0 |

## Interpretation
- The sample groups represent **different roles and decision purposes**. The means must **not** be used to rank cohorts or infer organizational talent quality.
- An n of 4 per cohort is too small for defensible generalizations or demographic fairness analysis.
- No data describe participant demographics, test validity, standard errors, reliability, or changes over time.
- None of these hypothetical indices are derived from licensed instruments.

## What HR could usefully request next
- A job-analysis matrix and validated score interpretation from an authorized test user.
- Standardized interview and work-sample scoring rules by role.
- Relevant performance evidence, participant consent and accessibility confirmation.
- A reviewed development plan based on job requirements and participant goals, not inferred personality deficits.

## Reporting recommendation
Use these aggregates only to demonstrate **descriptive reporting layout**. No selection, promotion, compensation, workforce or succession decisions should be based on this dataset.
