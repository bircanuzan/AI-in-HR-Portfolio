# ⭐ Psychometric Assessment & Talent Evaluation
### Portfolio Case Study | UAE Corporate Hiring, Promotion, Succession & Development

**Bircan Uzan | HumanEvoX**  
**Positioning:** Psychometric Assessment Practitioner · Occupational Test Interpretation · Talent Assessment Reporting · Talent Evaluation

> **Fictional and educational only.** This is an original simulated case, not an actual client engagement, assessment credential, administration of licensed tests, or evidence of past psychometric test-user practice. All 12 profiles and assessment indices are invented. **No BPS, SHL, Hogan or Saville accreditation is claimed.**

## The business challenge
A hypothetical UAE-based enterprise has **12 fictional participants** in three separate decisions: hire four Senior HR Analysts, consider four People Operations Managers for promotion, and review four potential HR Transformation Leads for succession.

An imagined qualified test provider supplies pre-scored results from **online and supervised onsite assessments**. The consultant is asked to **analyze, interpret with appropriate limitations, combine with other evidence, draft reports and advise HR**. This case does **not** include role-play facilitation, group-exercise observation or training delivery.

## What the project demonstrates
1. Defining assessment questions around validated job requirements.
2. Reviewing ability-related indices, personality/work-style indicators, and assessment administration conditions.
3. Checking data quality, provenance, validity and interpretability before reporting.
4. Analyzing fictional individuals and cohorts, recognizing limitations of small samples.
5. Triangulating test-style outputs with job-related interviews and work samples.
6. Drafting individual assessment interpretations and risk/development narratives.
7. Writing HR hiring, promotion and succession *decision-support* reports.
8. Planning candidate/HR feedback in virtual or onsite meetings.
9. Applying confidentiality, fairness, accessibility and human review controls.

## Illustrative finding
Fictional applicant C003 has arbitrary numerical/verbal/abstract indices of **68, 81, 72**. A demonstrative calculation gives **73.6/100** using hypothetical weights (40%/35%/25%). **This is not a normed psychometric score, percentile, validity-supported composite, or hiring threshold.** Fictional interview (86) and work-sample (83) indices suggest useful additional information. **Appropriate action:** request validated, role-related evidence before forming an employment recommendation; do not label the candidate from scores alone.

### Limitations and authorization
**No proprietary instruments, copyrighted test items or reports** are reproduced. Work-style preference measures are never treated as competence, clinical diagnoses or selection thresholds. A real psychometric practitioner must obtain relevant test-use qualifications, comply with publisher authorization and test manuals, check population-specific psychometric validity and reliability, and follow local privacy and anti-discrimination obligations. This repository **does not certify that those conditions have been met**.
