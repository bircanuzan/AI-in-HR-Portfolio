# Executive Director of AI — Stage Gate Decision Brief (Fictional)

**Case:** Group Finance invoice intake and exception routing.  
**Prepared by:** AI Lead, Systems & Process (portfolio role simulation).  
**Meeting purpose:** choose the next *discovery and controlled-design* investment, not approve production.

## Requested decision
1. Approve AP/Procurement/Finance/IT discovery and a 12-week evidence-gathering plan.
2. Assign Finance sponsor, AP process owner, Security/Data Privacy and ERP architect.
3. Authorise only synthetic/appropriately redacted sample testing pending data approvals.
4. Review six-opportunity pipeline monthly using common weighted scoring and risk hard stops.
5. Decline any agent design that could independently modify supplier bank records or release payments.

## Decision rationale
- **Business issue:** manual invoice keying, unstructured exception routing, weak visibility and potential approval ambiguity.
- **What we have:** original AS-IS/TO-BE design, user stories, 6-use-case backlog, hypothetical ROI and a reproducible **local rules-only routing demonstrator**.
- **What we do not have:** validated volume/time study, technical API/security clearance, extraction accuracy, buyer-funded quotation, live customer/system data, signed UAT or pilot outcomes.
- **Financial exposure:** base illustrative cost AED380k year one; value estimate AED497,664 **gross released capacity**, not cash. Downside case gross capacity value AED192k: a negative outcome after cost.

## Stage gates and owners
| Gate | Required artifact | Owner | Decision today |
|---|---|---|---|
| G0 Scope/owner | signed charter and AS-IS sample | AP + sponsor | REQUEST |
| G1 Requirements | FR traceability, controls and data approval | AP + Finance + Risk | PENDING |
| G2 Technology | OCR/sample quality, integration, security tests | IT architect + Data Privacy | PENDING |
| G3 UAT | critical scenarios and signatures | business owners | PENDING |
| G4 Pilot release | rollback, monitoring, access controls | control board | PENDING |
| G5 Scale / value | benchmarked actuals and Finance attestation | FP&A + Director of AI | PENDING |

## Top five risks and mitigations
| Risk | Likelihood/impact (illustrative) | Mitigation | Residual approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrong AI field extraction | M/H | provenance, confidence review, sample accuracy tests | AP owner |
| Duplicate payment/control weakness | M/Very High | hold duplicate, payment segregated from intake | Controller |
| Supplier banking fraud | M/Very High | out-of-band master-data verification | Security/Finance |
| Low adoption and shadow email process | H/M | champions, dual-run, training, SOP | AP + L&D |
| Estimated capacity not realized | H/M | matched time study, FP&A validation, phased go/no-go | Finance sponsor |

## Proposed Director of AI dashboard
Monthly portfolio review displays: status and stage gate of each use case; approved funding vs committed cost; decision owner; risk trend; training coverage; actual adoption; route and extraction QA; business-case variance; verified cash impact vs capacity released; next stop/continue decision. No invented actual KPI results.

## Interview presentation storyline
**Discover → prioritize → specify → control → test → adopt → measure.** Show working sample routing separately from the unexecuted enterprise steps. This demonstrates business-side AI systems leadership, not model engineering or company deployment.
