# 06 | Microsoft 365 Copilot readiness, licensing and security

**Important:** The interface and licensing structure evolve. Reconfirm entitlements and current product behavior before running a client workshop.

## Pre-flight tenant review (owned by client IT)
1. Confirm users' Microsoft 365 account and Copilot entitlement, available features and app build.
2. Identify the difference between basic Copilot Chat and paid Microsoft 365 Copilot work-grounded experiences; do not promise a tenant-wide feature without checking.
3. Validate supported Outlook, Teams, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OneDrive and SharePoint experiences in a test account with matching permissions.
4. Inventory Microsoft Graph/SharePoint/OneDrive access, link-sharing defaults, sensitivity labels, retention and data-loss policies.
5. Identify over-shared libraries, stale content and business owners for authoritative sources.
6. Confirm meeting transcription/recording, consent and retention policy for Teams scenarios.
7. Confirm approved external web use, AI use policy and restrictions on regulated personal/financial/employment data.
8. Agree escalation when a source is missing, not permissioned, contradictory or outdated.
9. Confirm change communications, IT support routes and learner accommodations.

## Human-in-the-loop boundaries
- Copilot is draft assistance and evidence navigation, **not policy approval**, legal advice, payroll decision or authorization to disclose confidential data.
- Check dates, amounts, names, context, citations and permissions manually.
- Require content owners to approve public and customer-facing outputs.
- Do not paste live confidential data into training materials.
- If a feature is missing in the tenant, use the *fictional exercise* without pretending the feature was demonstrated.

## Why data permissions matter
Microsoft describes work-grounded Microsoft 365 Copilot as working with organizational content the user can access. Existing oversharing and poor file hygiene can still affect output exposure; discovery must include approved permissions and source ownership.

## Suggested safeguards and responsibilities
| Risk | Test / action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Overshared SharePoint content | Permission review and pilot prompts against sample identities | IT/Privacy |
| Unsupported claim in email | Line-by-line source verification | Business owner |
| Wrong Excel figure | Recalculate on original source and formulas | Finance/Analyst |
| Incorrect Teams action | Compare with authorized transcript and attendees | Meeting owner |
| Outdated knowledge | Show current approved source + review date | Content owner |
| Poor adoption | Understand access, skill, workflow and value blockers | Adoption lead |
