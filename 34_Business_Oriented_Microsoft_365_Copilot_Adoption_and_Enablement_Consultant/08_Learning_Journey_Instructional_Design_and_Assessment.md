# 08 | Learning journey architecture, instructional design and skills assessment

## Competencies, not just tool familiarity
C1 — Frame a business task clearly: purpose, audience, known source, expected deliverable.
C2 — Choose a supported Copilot tool with appropriate licence and permission.
C3 — Compose a safe prompt and request transparency where a source is incomplete.
C4 — Evaluate accuracy, figures, citations, bias and confidentiality.
C5 — Revise the result and explain a human approval point.
C6 — Choose an adoption metric and apply the learning to one repeatable workflow.

## Audience-specific journey designs
| Persona | Microlearning | Guided practice | Final evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner user | 15-minute safe-use primer | Email + Word revision | Source-verified final draft |
| Power user / champion | 20-minute prompt critique | Teams actions + SharePoint version check | Peer-reviewed job aid |
| Finance / analytics professional | 20-minute data-check module | Excel variance + PowerPoint story | Correct reconciled numbers |
| Executive | 15-minute decision briefing | Scenario triage and business case | Signed pilot decision / open questions |
| Manager of teams | 20-minute coaching primer | Team use-case prioritization | Two measured adoption experiments |

## Content storyboard example: 12-minute "Meeting to accountable action"
- **Minute 0–2:** Why notes fail in remote teams; frame work outcome.
- **Minute 2–4:** Show a fictional meeting-note source and consent/permissions.
- **Minute 4–7:** Construct a request to extract *only* explicitly assigned actions.
- **Minute 7–9:** Compare proposed output to source; highlight one wrong inference.
- **Minute 9–11:** Learner repairs the action list and describes human verification.
- **Minute 11–12:** One-question reflection and action pledge.

## Sample knowledge and practical check
**Question:** If meeting notes do not name an owner, should Copilot choose one from context? **Correct:** No; mark TBD, then request clarification.
**Practical challenge:** Revise a fictional policy email without inventing dates or conditions.
**Scoring:** 6 required checks in [KPI and quality scorecard](07_Adoption_KPIs_Quality_Scorecard_and_Value_Case.md); a major privacy error fails the activity.
**Reflection:** Give one scenario where a rule/ordinary template beats AI.

## Learning products
Facilitator run-of-show, participant worksheet, job aids, prompt card, scenario/source pack, assessment rubric, champion FAQ, executive deck storyboard and 2-week follow-up clinic protocol.

No recorded workshop, certification completion, actual participant assessment, SCORM package, commercial facilitation or production adoption outcome is claimed.
