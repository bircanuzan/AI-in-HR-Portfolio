# 02 | Practical workshops — facilitator guides

## Workshop A: Everyday business users (90 minutes)
| Time | Facilitation | Participant output | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Welcome, objectives and safe-use boundaries | One recurring pain point | Sensitive data excluded |
| 10–20 | Explain task, context, sources and verification | Reusable prompt template | States audience and output |
| 20–40 | Paired exercise: Outlook + Word | Draft + human-edited version | No unsupported promises |
| 40–55 | Teams actions to task list | Owner / action / due-date tracker | Unclear owners flagged |
| 55–70 | Excel or business memo analysis | Source-backed insight summary | Figures verified independently |
| 70–80 | OneDrive / SharePoint source finding | Document version validation | Permissions respected |
| 80–90 | Apply to own role: 1-week experiment | Measurable behaviour commitment | Follow-up owner identified |

**Facilitator direction:** invite participants to critique outputs; avoid claiming that output quality is necessarily higher than manual work. If features unavailable, use the supplied offline fictional documents and discuss evaluation rather than pretend a live demo occurred.

## Workshop B: Champions and change agents (75 minutes)
- 10 min: business priorities and safe use
- 20 min: choose departmental use cases and write paired prompts
- 15 min: red-team AI outputs for invented facts, source gaps and oversharing
- 15 min: set up peer support rhythm, office hours, microlearning and escalation
- 15 min: define adoption/quality signals and champion responsibilities

## Workshop C: senior managers / directors / VPs (60 minutes)
Use [Executive workshop](04_Executive_Leadership_Workshop_and_Value_Story.md) — focus on business decisions, governance, role modelling, adoption and accountable KPIs, not app-by-app feature tutorials.

## Facilitator talk track for one demonstration
"We'll use a fictional policy email. First define the audience, objective, facts to include and boundaries. Generate a first draft only if the tenant enables that experience. Then compare every claim with the source, improve tone and clarify actions. Copilot gives a *draft*, not approval."

## Before-session readiness checklist
Validate attendee accounts/permissions, app availability, storage location, sandbox sample files, confidentiality policy, accessibility needs, screen sharing and recording policy, fallback slides and post-session evaluation tool.

## After-session checklist
Collect participant confidence, task completion and feedback; assign one safe use case per user; ask managers to enable practice time; schedule 2-week adoption clinic. No actual delivered events are claimed here.
