# 01 | Discover business needs — before opening Copilot

## Fictional client
Northbridge Services (illustrative 1,200-person professional services company) wants less time spent reworking documents, meetings, internal search and executive reporting. No stakeholder interviews have actually been conducted.

## 40-minute discovery interview guide
1. What decision or communication is delayed each week? Who is accountable?
2. Which existing workflow uses Outlook, Teams, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OneDrive or SharePoint?
3. How frequently does the work recur, and what are quality and compliance consequences of a wrong output?
4. What source data is approved for AI use? Who owns the file and permissions?
5. What is the true baseline: total elapsed time, human effort, revisions, error rates and recipient satisfaction?
6. Who can test Copilot in the current tenant and what licence/user profile exists?
7. What would prove impact after four weeks and what might cause the use case to be rejected?
8. What would be a successful executive demonstration versus frontline demonstration?

## Use-case prioritisation — synthetic assessments only
| Persona | Need | Proposed workflow | Candidate applications | Benefit hypothesis | Risk and owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CHRO | Prepare staff policy communication | Draft using an approved policy, review wording and citations | Word + Outlook | Less writing/rework | HR policy owner must approve |
| CFO | Explain variance trends for directors | Explore fictional table, check formulas and create narrative | Excel + PowerPoint | Clearer briefing preparation | Finance validates numerical claims |
| VP Sales | Turn a client meeting into follow-up | Extract actions from authorized transcript, draft message | Teams + Outlook | Fewer dropped actions | Meeting consent/permissions |
| Operations Lead | Find the current SOP | Locate authorized source and compare versions | OneDrive + SharePoint | Less searching and stale content | Content owner verifies current version |
| New employee | Learn how to ask better questions | Safe prompt structuring and verification | Copilot Chat + Word | Higher confidence and autonomy | IT/privacy define permitted data |

## Simple scoring method
Give 0–3 points each for **business pain**, **frequency**, **data readiness**, **expected measurable benefit**, and **stakeholder sponsorship**. Separately mark risk **low / medium / high**. A high-risk case cannot be fast-tracked merely because it has a high value score.

## Deliverable: one-page opportunity brief
- Problem and current process (one paragraph)
- Users, process owner and supported tenant/licence
- App-specific workflow and data/source permissions
- Two baseline measures and defined validation approach
- Outcome hypothesis and human review checkpoints
- Accessibility and change impacts
- Decision: pilot / investigate / reject

**Result:** clear adoption backlog and workshop segmentation, not a generic list of prompts.
