# Northbridge Services — fictional business case

## Problem
Managers spend time recapping meetings and repackaging business updates. Source documents are distributed across email and shared storage, with unclear version ownership.

## Trial hypothesis
Test AI-assisted first drafts of permitted meeting actions, policy communications and board-pack outlines against manual baseline and quality checks.

## Synthetic workload assumptions
- 300 eligible recurring tasks per month.
- 18 minutes average manual preparation per task, subject to validation.
- A proposed 6 minutes **net** improvement per task **only if actually proven**, including human correction.
- 300 × 6 / 60 = 30 hypothetical hours/month available for other work.
- No financial savings are claimed because loaded cost, licence expense, support cost and genuine budget realization are unknown.

## Controls
Approve information access, protect employee records, check claims and maintain human sign-off. No actual pilot, ROI result or enterprise access is asserted.
