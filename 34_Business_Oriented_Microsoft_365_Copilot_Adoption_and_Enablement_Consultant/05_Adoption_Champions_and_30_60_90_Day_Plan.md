# 05 | Adoption and behaviour-change plan — 30 / 60 / 90 days

## Day 0–30: discover, prepare and pilot
- Identify five business functions and role-specific high-frequency tasks.
- Check licence, Microsoft 365 readiness, SharePoint/OneDrive permissions, security and information governance with IT.
- Baseline task-level accuracy, minutes, rework and confidence for at least two eligible tasks.
- Design executive, champion and end-user learning paths and translated use cases.
- Recruit champions and define a contact/support model.
- Pilot small cohorts only with permitted synthetic or approved material.

**Gate:** business and technical owners approve use cases, evaluation approach and training readiness.

## Day 31–60: enable, practise and gather evidence
- Deliver proposed hands-on labs, job aids and office hours; include human verification habits.
- Run paired task tests, capture completed task evidence and learner self-assessment.
- Monitor activation and repeat usage using *appropriately permissioned* Copilot Analytics or admin reports.
- Fix content gaps and reinforce managers' role modelling.
- Investigate weak adoption: no licence, access problems, task mismatch, poor quality or unclear benefits.

**Gate:** no critical privacy or accuracy concern; at least one use case has an agreed, reproducible quality assessment.

## Day 61–90: scale selectively
- Expand successful roles and tasks using champions.
- Refresh prompts and support material as product features change.
- Hold functional value reviews; check actual time/cost and rework.
- Separate **training attended**, **usage**, **competent use** and **sustained business outcome**.
- Confirm service ownership, content publishing and refresher cadence.

**Gate:** business sponsor signs off which interventions scale, improve or stop.

| User metrics and quality | Analytics lead | Adoption lead + quality reviewers | IT and HR |

## Champion role
Run 20-minute weekly drop-in support, share safe examples, capture misconceptions, propose one documented improvement and escalate limitations. Champions do not become de facto privacy, IT or employment decision approvers.

## Accessibility and inclusion
Provide accessible documents, keyboard-friendly materials, plain English prompts, varied entry levels, realistic non-technical examples and practice time. No languages other than English are required or claimed in this case.

## Deliverable
A self-sustaining adoption plan with accountable owners, learning support, success measures and staged release gates. **Proposed, not delivered.**
