# 04 | Executive workshop: practical AI productivity, not software training

## Audience
CHRO, CFO, business unit MD/VP, CIO/IT, change sponsor and selected operational owners.

## 60-minute workshop
- **00–10 — Executive outcome**: top three business bottlenecks, not app features.
- **10–20 — Fictional case**: prepare a board update and policy communication from trusted sources using approved Copilot capabilities.
- **20–35 — Guided choices**: assess a function-by-function opportunity matrix: value, risk, licence readiness, user capability and data quality.
- **35–45 — Responsible use**: show incomplete/incorrect draft and ask who signs off, how to verify and where access controls apply.
- **45–55 — Financial discipline**: baseline handling time, QA/rework, usage/spend and conservative possible capacity release. Separate measured capacity value from realised savings.
- **55–60 — Decision ask**: approve owner, pilot population, governance, success criteria and the 30-day next decision gate.

## Eight-slide executive storyline (PowerPoint storyboard)
1. "Where work gets stuck" — workflow friction map.
2. "Who benefits" — frontline, manager, business operations examples.
3. "What Copilot can support" — cases and licence caveats.
4. "Where AI should not decide" — financial, employment and legal approvals.
5. "What changed in the pilot" — placeholder for **future** measured outcomes.
6. "Adoption requires behaviour change" — champions, leadership and quality.
7. "Value and cost model" — verified sources, licence, support and time.
8. "Approve or stop" — sponsor decisions and ownership.

## Consultant executive narrative
"The value isn't how many prompts employees type. It's whether a defined task becomes faster *without reducing accuracy, trust, access control or customer outcomes*. Start with approved use cases. Measure the process, not just app activity, and stop use cases whose review cost cancels out the benefit."

## Example business value model (not observed)
A team handles 300 approved recurring tasks each month. If *verified* average net time saved is 6 minutes after review, possible released capacity is 30 hours/month. This is not 30 billable hours or actual budget savings. Finance must confirm realization, licence costs and displaced activities before any ROI statement.

## Leadership decision log
Record goal, scope, data owner, technical readiness, proposed users, executive sponsor, delegated sign-offs, evidence gaps, next action and date. This is a template; no real workshop or approval is claimed.
