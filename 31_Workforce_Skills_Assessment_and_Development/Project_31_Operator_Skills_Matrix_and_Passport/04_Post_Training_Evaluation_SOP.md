# Post-training evaluation SOP — illustrative template

## Purpose
Verify whether competency gaps close through observed, job-relevant performance rather than training attendance alone.

## Inputs
Approved task SOP, baseline rating, skill target, assessor rubric, evidence template, safety-critical gates, training plan and qualified assessor.

## Workflow
1. **Approve scope:** identify employee, task, version, target proficiency and required technical sign-off.
2. **Deliver intervention:** record coaching, hands-on practice or certification activity; record completion without treating it as proof of competence.
3. **Schedule reassessment:** agree appropriate practice period, assessor and realistic task conditions.
4. **Observe:** use the approved rubric and record independent evidence (task result, errors, interventions, date).
5. **Compare:** show baseline versus post-training level and remaining gap per competency.
6. **Decide:** mark achieved, further practice, or blocked by mandatory technical/safety requirement.
7. **Authorize:** qualified line/technical assessor signs; HR/learning team maintains the passport.
8. **Review retention:** retest after an agreed interval and monitor for drift.

## Audit and QA checklist
- [ ] Approved test and rubrics
- [ ] Correct SOP and safety requirements
- [ ] Named qualified assessor
- [ ] Baseline evidence retained
- [ ] Training and practice evidence retained
- [ ] Post-training evidence retained
- [ ] Safety approval separately documented
- [ ] Skill passport revision dated
- [ ] Follow-up review scheduled

**Demonstration status:** SOP template only. No technical validation or live implementation.
