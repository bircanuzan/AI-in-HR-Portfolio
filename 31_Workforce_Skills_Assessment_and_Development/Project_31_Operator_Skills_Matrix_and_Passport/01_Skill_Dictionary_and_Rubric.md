# Skill dictionary and observable proficiency rubric

**Role family:** Production operator on a fictional assembly line. **Assessment method:** job observation, practical task and approved evidence sheet.

| Level | Observable anchor | Evidence example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Guided | Performs part of the task only with direct help | Assessor records which steps required assistance |
| 2 — Developing | Performs standard steps with intermittent prompts | Observed work sample and error / prompt log |
| 3 — Independent | Completes the approved routine without prompting | Practical observation against signed SOP |
| 4 — Coach / improve | Consistently meets standard and can coach peers | Repeat observations plus supervisor-approved demonstration |

## Competency dictionary

| Competency | Expected demonstration | Illustrative target | Evidence | Safety / review control |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| PPE and isolation awareness | Names, selects and uses relevant controls per approved SOP | 4 | Observed safety walkthrough | Mandatory technical/safety sign-off |
| Visual quality inspection | Identifies sample defects against approved acceptance criteria | 3 | Defect identification work sample | SME approves examples and thresholds |
| Workstation changeover | Completes standard changeover in prescribed sequence | 3 | Timed observed task and checklist | Approved tools and supervision required |
| Production escalation | Recognizes and escalates out-of-limit conditions | 3 | Situational scenario and verbal walkthrough | Escalation route confirmed by supervisor |

## Scoring safeguards
- Capture observation date, assessor, task version, evidence reference and applicable SOP identifier.
- Record **Not assessed** rather than assigning a guessed rating.
- Do not permit independent authorization if a mandatory safety check is failed, even if a composite score is high.
- Targets and tests are illustrative placeholders pending technical SME validation.
