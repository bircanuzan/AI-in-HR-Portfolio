# AI & BI Centre of Excellence — fictional enterprise design

**Mission:** convert approved insurance-business problems into measurable, governed analytics and AI use cases, without delegating irreversible decisions to unsupported models.

| Role | Accountability |
|---|---|
| Executive sponsor / COO or CIO | business funding, prioritization and decision rights |
| AI/BI CoE lead | roadmap, portfolio value, delivery dependencies, vendor management |
| Claims Product Owner | outcome definitions, queue operations, sign-off, overrides |
| Data Owner / Data Steward | semantic contract, lineage, data quality and stewardship |
| Data Engineering / Cloud Architecture | integration, pipelines, access, reliability |
| Model Risk / Actuarial / Risk Management | independent validation, monitoring thresholds, model approval |
| Privacy, Security & Legal | UAE requirements assessment, lawful basis, access, vendor and incident gates |
| BI Lead | certified semantic models, governed self-service, dashboard accessibility |
| Frontline claims reviewers | human approval, escalation, error reporting and structured feedback |

## Portfolio intake and scoring
Each use case requires problem statement, available source/quality evidence, lawful basis, stakeholder, security classification, human decision owner, indicative value hypothesis, cost estimate, risk level and a **stop condition**. Score separately on value, feasibility, data readiness, risk and change effort; reject use cases lacking source data or clear accountability.

## 90-day illustrative execution plan
- **Days 1–30:** insurer-specific discovery; current architecture and maturity; data governance charter, owners, baseline KPI definitions, use-case prioritization and privacy scoping. *Exit:* signed problem definition, not model deployment.
- **Days 31–60:** source-to-target data mappings, data-quality prototype, dashboard semantics, synthetic model baseline, UAT pack, independent evaluation plan, vendor due diligence. *Exit:* validation committee permission to pursue a limited pilot.
- **Days 61–90:** proposed shadow-mode pilot using an approved environment, human-review workflow, feedback collection, monitoring and training materials, financial sensitivity review. *Exit:* approve / rework / stop based on documented evidence.

## Governance cadence and artifacts
- Weekly delivery / incident board; monthly data council and CoE roadmap review; quarterly executive value/risk review.
- Required registers: data products/owners, use case intake, model inventory/version, incident and overrides, approvals, data-quality breaches, vendor risks, benefits assumptions and decision log.
- Team training is end-user enablement and controls, not evidence of this case having trained any insurer staff.

**Status:** design documentation only. No CoE, client board or Azure deployment is claimed operational.
