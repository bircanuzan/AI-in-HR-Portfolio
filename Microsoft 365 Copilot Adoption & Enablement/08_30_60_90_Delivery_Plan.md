# Fictional 30 / 60 / 90-day Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption programme
**Organisation:** UAE services group, entirely hypothetical. **Programme owner:** business transformation manager. **Tenant owner:** Microsoft 365 administrator. **Executive sponsor:** COO.

## Day 1–30: Discover and validate
- Interview 2–3 stakeholders per function (HR, Finance, Operations, Executive Office).
- AS-IS task mapping, task timing and quality baselines with permitted fictional/dummy data.
- Confirm Copilot entitlement, product experiences and access with M365 administrator.
- Map privacy-sensitive data and security restrictions with DPO/IT.
- Rank use cases by value, feasibility, data readiness, training needs and risk.
- Deliverables: inventory, functional use-case matrix, initial risk register, learning needs/role-persona plan.

## Day 31–60: Train, practice, observe
- Pilot with a clearly defined eligible user group and structured learning sessions.
- **End-user labs:** prompt design, source review and workplace task.
- **Champions:** peer coaching, support escalation and local examples.
- **Director/VP/MD session:** decision support, accountable governance and resource decisions.
- Capture pre/post knowledge checks, sample task outputs and approved usage telemetry.
- Deliverables: workshop guides, QA rubric, privacy-safe feedback, support and issue log.

## Day 61–90: Reinforce and scale only when justified
- Assess whether task quality and time improved against baseline.
- Review adoption, accessibility, permissions, complaints and potential business harm.
- Select stronger use cases, retire low-value or risky ones, update supporting material.
- Build a sustainable champions community and monthly feedback/refresh cycle.
- Present executive decision memo: scale, adjust or stop, based on evidence.
- Deliverables: executive KPI pack, benefits hypothesis vs measured outcomes, adoption backlog and risks.

## Example go/no-go decision
Go only if licenses, permissions, privacy/security, appropriate pilot data, functional acceptance and human validation are evidenced. HOLD if uncertain. NO-GO for uncontrolled data exposure or any planned autonomous material adverse HR/finance decision without oversight.
