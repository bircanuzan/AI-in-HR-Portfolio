# Tools, platform readiness, adoption governance and business–IT coordination
**This is a business consultant's operating-model design, not a production tenant build.** Specific features depend on licence, tenant, country and product change.

## Business-facing tool map
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot / Copilot Chat:** role-oriented assistance with approved context and source verification; access depends on licence and tenant.
- **Outlook:** draft and revise stakeholder communications and messages where functionality is supported.
- **Teams:** meeting recap and follow-up where subscription, recording/transcript consent and permissions support it.
- **Word:** controlled drafting and document summarisation with source review.
- **Excel:** financial narrative and analysis; **always recalculate or independently check numerics**.
- **PowerPoint:** turn verified business material into draft narratives and presentation structures.
- **OneDrive / SharePoint:** governed content access and information discovery, subject to access entitlements.
- **Power Automate:** potential approved workflow handoffs with human approvals; no actual live flow built here.
- **Copilot Studio:** optional agent extension concept with governance and identity checks; reference design only.
- **Power BI, Excel/Power Query:** possible business KPI reporting from authorised sources.
- **Microsoft Purview:** security/data governance option requiring technical/IT oversight, not personally configured in this study.
- **ServiceNow / HRIS:** business workflow integration concepts, not a tested tenant connector.
- **ChatGPT, Make, n8n, Zapier:** familiar related AI/workflow concepts; not proof of Microsoft 365 Copilot platform tenure.

## Pre-pilot readiness gates
| Gate | Accountable owner | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed/supported product experiences | Microsoft 365 admin | Confirm licenses, app versions and scope |
| SharePoint / OneDrive permissions | Data owners and security | Sensitive content visibility audit |
| Privacy and retention | DPO and counsel | Data-use mapping, retention, consent where applicable |
| User and group policy | Identity administrator | RBAC/MFA and permitted access |
| Approved test environment | IT product team | Safe fictional demo materials and participant access |
| Approved scenarios | Function leads | Business problem, real objective and verification rubric |
| Human oversight | Process owners | Error routing and decision sign-off |
| Measurement | Analytics lead | Baseline, user privacy, accessible KPI sources |
| Sponsor & communications | Executive/change lead | Stakeholder sign-off and champion plan |

## Governance concerns addressed explicitly
- **Oversharing:** AI may surface content a user already has access to, so access cleanup and data classification must precede rollout.
- **Inaccurate outputs:** require source-checking, especially financial numbers, people/HR decisions and policy interpretations.
- **Sensitive information:** never use real employee records in public screenshots or unapproved demonstration environments.
- **Automated decisions:** Copilot-generated recommendations are drafts; high-impact personnel and financial actions remain human accountable.
- **Accessibility:** allow alternative exercises and non-AI route; measure who is excluded by device, language or permissions.
- **Feature truth:** verify in the real tenant and document a fallback if a requested Copilot experience is unavailable.

**Not evidenced:** Microsoft Certified Trainer, Copilot tenant administration, live Power Automate connections, Purview configuration, Copilot Studio deployment, or measured customer adoption.
