# AI CoE and Portfolio Governance — Intake, Qualification, RACI and Decision Rights

## Intake requirements
Demand owner, affected business process, AI or IT classification, strategic theme, business problem, benefits hypothesis, quantification method, financial envelope, security/privacy criticality, dependencies, sponsor, target timeframe and evidence attachment references.

## Demand prioritisation (proposed only)
Weighted criteria: strategic fit 30%, quantified business value 25%, implementation feasibility 20%, responsible AI and delivery readiness 15%, resource availability 10%. Criteria are scored 1–5; weighted score = sum(weight × score). Scoring supports review; it does not replace management judgement or formal approvals.

## Dispositions
**Approved** requires recorded forum decision, finance and risk sign-off, and conversion target; **Deferred** requires reason, owner and revisit date; **Rejected** requires rationale and records retained for audit. The pipeline remains a reportable asset for every outcome.

## Decision-rights/RACI (R = Responsible; A = Accountable; C = Consulted; I = Informed)
| Activity | AI PMO | VP Portfolio | AI CoE | Product Owner | Finance | Security/Privacy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intake completeness | R | I | C | A | C | C |
| Technical feasibility | C | I | A/R | C | I | C |
| Priority ranking | R | A | C | C | C | C |
| Funding approval | C | A | C | C | R | I |
| AI risk/security review | C | I | C | A | I | R |
| Demand-to-project conversion | R | A | C | R | C | C |
| UAT/release readiness | C | I | R | A | I | C |
| Benefits acceptance | R | A | C | R | C | I |
| Product decommissioning | R | A | C | R | C | C |

## Governance forums
- Weekly intake triage (new and ageing demands, eligibility, completeness).
- Fortnightly CoE technical and responsible AI review.
- Monthly portfolio investment board (priority, finance, deferrals, business case evidence).
- Weekly delivery RAID / UAT readiness.
- Monthly benefits and product EOL review.
- Ad hoc incident escalation for severe AI, privacy and compliance issues.

## Responsible AI minimum controls
Human owner, intended purpose, prohibited uses, data protection assessment, security approval, model/data lineage, performance testing, user escalation, post-release monitoring and audit logs. High-impact use cases require additional independent review before production promotion.
