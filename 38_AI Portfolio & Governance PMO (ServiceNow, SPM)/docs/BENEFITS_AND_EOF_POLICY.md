# Financial Governance, Benefits Realisation and Product End-of-Life

## Financial governance
Keep **budget/funding approvals**, **cost plans**, **actual expenditure**, **forecast at completion** and **benefit values** separate. The finance owner validates all amounts before portfolio approval. A funding shortfall is a flag for review, **not** evidence the investment was overspent.

## Benefits tracking method
1. Record baseline process KPI, unit of measure, source and owner.
2. State intended benefit (time, quality, risk or cost reduction) and method to validate.
3. Tie expected benefits to a demand and, following conversion, a project/product.
4. Review at close and again post-implementation; distinguish forecast from realised evidence.
5. Record adverse results, new operating costs, assumptions and lessons learned.

## Product handover readiness
Product owner and service-level commitments; runbook, UAT approvals, model/version/data owner register, privacy/security approvals, ongoing monitoring/incident process, cost centre, benefits ownership, rollback and revalidation trigger.

## End-of-life (EOL) controls
Identify redundant product -> business/product owner sign-off -> dependency mapping -> preservation/retention review -> communications and migration -> revoke access/API keys -> stop model and jobs -> archive financial and audit evidence -> benefit and decommission record -> post-EOL check.

## Portfolio maturity measures (proposed)
% active demands fully classified; % funded cases with finance approval; % projects with current milestones; % open critical RAID over SLA; % live products with UAT/security evidence; % benefit owners reporting actual outcomes. These are management controls, not certified AI maturity scores.
