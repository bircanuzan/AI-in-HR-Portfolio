# Project Charter — Enterprise AI Portfolio & Governance PMO

## Mandate
Design a repeatable SPM-aligned portfolio management system for AI demands, pilots, delivery projects and products. Maintain evidence of strategy alignment, responsible AI, viability, funding, approval and benefits. Create decision-ready reporting for VP Portfolio & Governance and the AI CoE.

## Sponsor and stakeholders
VP Portfolio & Governance (funding decisions); AI CoE Lead (AI technical feasibility); Product Management (value, product transition); Enterprise PMO (stage gates); Finance (funding and cost validation); Security/Privacy/Legal (risk sign-offs); data/model owners (dependencies); independent assurance (audit quality).

## Success measures (illustrative targets, not achieved client results)
- 100% of new AI demands have accountable owner, category, business case reference, strategic alignment and risk classification.
- 100% of approved investments have a named approver, approval date and available funding record.
- Every material AI pilot has security and responsible AI review before live use.
- Weekly RAID review, daily record update expectations and monthly benefits review.
- All demand dispositions (including rejection and deferral) are retained with decision rationale.

## Six-stage delivery
1. **Discovery:** AI demand taxonomy, stakeholder interviews, portfolio baselines, current-state intake and governance issues.
2. **Design:** demand workflow, decision rights, classification, scorecard, governance forums, portfolio data dictionary, BRD/UAT requirements.
3. **Build demonstration:** synthetic SPM-aligned CSV records, demand-to-project/product crosswalk, cost/funding records, RAID, evidence register, code checks.
4. **Assure:** test missing evidence, cost-overrun, overdue milestones, invalid disposition and duplicate IDs; document UAT and security reviews.
5. **Report:** produce governance committee pack, health/maturity indicators, exceptions and funding decision materials.
6. **Operate / improve:** daily accuracy controls, weekly forum cadence, monthly benefit updates, change control, closure lessons learned and decommissioning.

## Lifecycle approval gates
G0 Intake complete -> G1 Qualified (business value and feasibility) -> G2 Prioritised (forum ranking) -> G3 Funded/approved (finance + sponsor) -> G4 UAT/security/release readiness -> G5 Closure acceptance -> G6 Benefits review / product end-of-life.

## Outputs
Pipeline reports, prioritised demand backlog, board decisions, funding/cost reconciliations, project health report, traceable approvals, audit-ready document index, benefit hypotheses vs outcomes, product transition and EOL records.

## Explicit scope boundary
No real instance administrator access, ServiceNow configuration, business-system integration, company approval, live AI model or regulatory assurance is claimed. The scripts are process demonstration and can form requirements for an authorised SPM implementation.
