# Strategic account growth, pre-sales and CHRO storytelling

## Account plan — fictional Northbridge Group
**Sponsor map:** CHRO executive sponsor; CFO/COO benefit owners; HR Analytics and IT architecture buyers/influencers; procurement/legal/security decision gates. Maintain relationship and stakeholder map with explicit next meeting, risk and decision.

**Land and expand:**
1. Phase 0: paid discovery / KPI strategy.
2. Phase 1: architecture design and approved pilot for workforce composition insights.
3. Phase 2: contingent expansion to learning/skills/recruiting insights, if data readiness and business case pass.
4. Phase 3: data-literacy academy, governance support, new cohorts/geographies; only after usage and outcomes substantiate renewal.

## Proposal / bid strategy
Discovery objectives; two alternatives; measurable acceptance; named implementation partner responsibilities; SAP BDC licensing dependency; out-of-scope services; assumptions and risk; indicative effort estimates; decision calendar and differentiated business value.

## Client platform and remote collaboration
PowerPoint sponsor deck; Excel KPI catalogue and assumption model; Miro prioritization; Qualtrics surveys; Flourish *only de-identified* view of trends; Scoro tasks, capacity and delivery milestones; 3n Strategy Customer Platform publishing as a proposed handoff (not accessed).

## 8-slide executive presentation storyboard
1. **Decision:** what a trusted people analytics programme changes.
2. **Business problem:** fragmented numbers and slow leadership action.
3. **Discovery evidence:** data-source map and agreed decision bottlenecks.
4. **Vision:** outcomes, value principles and governance.
5. **Measures:** KPI dictionary, adoption and ownership.
6. **SAP alignment:** BDC/People Intelligence/SAC solution options, prerequisites and limits.
7. **Roadmap:** realistic phased plan, budget assumptions, risks and enablement.
8. **Decision ask:** approve discovery scope, two priority use cases, sponsor and technical-owner appointments.

## Engagement governance
Weekly client working group; fortnightly sponsor progress; monthly account opportunity review; quarterly benefits/value review. Record signed acceptance and adoption separately from opportunity pipeline. No actual account contracts, clients or renewal results are implied.
