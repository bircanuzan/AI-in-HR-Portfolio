# Executive vision-setting and discovery

## Fictional client: Northbridge Group
A 9,000-person, five-country enterprise wants trusted data for attrition, critical skills, succession and hiring capacity decisions. Current operational extracts across HRIS, ATS, payroll and spreadsheets disagree; the company is *not* claimed as an actual customer.

## 90-minute CHRO workshop
- 0-15 — agree decisions to improve, sponsors, business outcomes and success signals.
- 15-35 — Miro decision mapping; where data and decisions fail and who owns each step.
- 35-55 — data-source interviews covering SuccessFactors modules, payroll, ATS, mobility and learning; catalog lineage and privacy issues.
- 55-75 — impact-versus-feasibility voting for two use cases; review qualitative notes and quantitative baselines.
- 75-90 — priority, named sponsor, success metrics, constraints, discovery follow-ups and go/no-go.

## Interview guide
What question cannot the CHRO answer today? Which report is distrusted and why? Are hires, exits and headcount defined consistently across geographies and business units? What is the reporting latency? Who can approve access to pay and employee data? Which senior managers will act on dashboard insights? What data cannot leave a region?

## Mock Qualtrics survey (design only, not deployed)
Rate 1-5: data confidence; report timeliness; metric consistency; analytical capability; usefulness of existing dashboards; ability to take action. Open questions: top decisions, exceptions, change constraints, desired story view. Avoid names and employee-sensitive attributes in survey responses.

## Vision output
**Trusted, actionable and privacy-respecting people intelligence** — governed workforce KPIs joined to business questions with clear decision ownership and adoption measures.

## Practical artefacts
- Stakeholder RACI; decisions-to-KPIs matrix; discovery notes taxonomy
- Miro workshop swimlanes and source-map specification
- Qualitative theme coding (missing context, duplicate metric definition, unreliable source, access restriction)
- Proposed 30/60/90 day prioritisation workshop; executive presentation outline

No workshops or surveys in this document were conducted with an actual client.
