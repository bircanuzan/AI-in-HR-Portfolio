# Transformation roadmap, value realization and adoption

## Four-stage plan
**0-30 days — discover:** interviews, Qualtrics survey design, as-is data landscape, KPI glossary, sponsor vision, tool and licence due diligence.
**31-60 days — prioritise and design:** solution-alignment workshops with Datasphere/SAC consultants, mature KPIs, privacy review, mock SAC story specification, business case.
**61-90 days — prove business value:** technical team implements a controlled sample when environments/approvals exist; HR team performs UAT and validates manager usability; identify source and access issues.
**91-180 days — adopt and grow:** train CHRO/HRBP stakeholders, publish documented glossary and support ownership, measure usage and decisions; expand only if data trust and value pass gates.

## Investment-case structure (illustrative)
Baseline each report's preparation effort, dispute rate, time to decision and intervention adoption. Quantified hypotheses:
- Potential report time released = confirmed reports/month * proven time saved/report * validated labor cost.
- Incremental investment = licence/consumption + technical delivery + change/training + ongoing governance.
- Net value = finance-approved benefit minus all validated incremental costs, including opportunity costs.
No client savings are claimed until actually measured and signed off.

## Change and enablement
Executive narrative, manager training, role-specific story walkthroughs, certified KPI glossary, accessibility and adoption questions, learning circles, office hours, monthly decision/quality reviews.

## Risk register (examples)
Unknown SuccessFactors master data quality; licence entitlement mismatch; inconsistent employee effective dates; unsupported hierarchy; privacy and works council constraints; self-service without analytical literacy; BI costs underbudgeted. Owners: SAP leads/HR data/privacy/finance/programme manager.

## Success metrics
Approved KPI definitions, reconciliation pass rate, sponsor-approved decisions influenced, monthly active authorized users, adoption by persona, reporting latency and cost of ownership; all start with a measured baseline.
