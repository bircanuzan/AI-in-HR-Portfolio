# 01 — Strategic discovery and executive workshop

**Customer:** fictional GulfConnect Group (UAE multi-business enterprise). **Business objective:** fewer manual hand-offs across HR, CRM and finance; trusted management reporting; secure AI assistance.

## 90-minute workshop
- **00–15: Strategic goals.** CIO/CFO/CHRO prioritize customer service, processing delay and reporting reliability.
- **15–35: Application portfolio.** Document Oracle HCM/ERP, CRM, legacy portal, integrations, vendor limits and true systems of record.
- **35–55: Data and risk.** Confirm residency, sensitive employee/customer data, identities, controls and required audit trails.
- **55–75: Reference architecture and options.** Compare integration patterns, PaaS modernization and data/AI routes.
- **75–90: Decision and sponsor.** Select a 12-week *paid discovery + PoV* and define sign-off owners.

## Prioritized needs and acceptance
| ID | Need | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Oracle/CRM interoperability | Versioned API contract, synthetic trace and security check |
| BR-02 | Data analytics | Documented metric definitions and lineage |
| BR-03 | Legacy application modernization | 6R comparison and nonfunctional criteria |
| BR-04 | Governed AI | Tested retrieval, abstention and human approval |
| BR-05 | Reliable integrations | Retry, idempotency, monitoring and failure-mode test |
| BR-06 | Cost credibility | Parameterized estimate and exclusions |

## Discovery questions
What Oracle modules and supported interfaces are available? What CRM platform? Which functions need subsecond responses versus nightly refresh? What is peak volume and p95 latency? Which internal team operates Azure landing zone, IAM, Key Vault and SRE? Which security reviews, legal constraints and procurement gates are mandatory? What measurable baseline and success threshold can business owners sign?

## Outputs and pursuit gate
Deliver business capability map, stakeholders/RACI, NFR catalogue, target-state choices, PoV backlog, risks, estimated order of magnitude and decision paper. **Pursue** when sponsor, dataset, supported APIs, budget and key control owners are identified. Otherwise recommend paid discovery before fixed delivery pricing.

## Executive talk track
"We preserve your Oracle core as a trusted system of record. We introduce controlled integrations and decision-ready data, and prove one high-value workflow before expanding. The design reduces unnecessary platform change and gives delivery teams clear acceptance tests."
