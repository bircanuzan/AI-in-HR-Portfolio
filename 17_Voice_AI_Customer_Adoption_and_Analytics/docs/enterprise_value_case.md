# Executive value case — hypothetical UAE enterprise example

**No client results or verified ROI are claimed.** Use this as an interview case to show how a value hypothesis would be measured and sold.

## Problem framing
A large shared-services or customer operations function handles recurring questions through human contact centre staff. Possible friction: wait times, after-hours demand, language coverage, repeated questions and agent training burden.

## Discovery and decision process
1. Segment intents by volume, sensitivity, complexity, authentication and compliance requirements.
2. Prioritize low-risk, repeatable interactions with a clear approved answer and human fallback.
3. Agree pilot metrics with operations, InfoSec, data/privacy and finance.
4. Establish baseline human-handled cost and quality; separately price speech, LLM, telephony, vendor seats, integration, operational staffing and review costs.
5. Compare actual incremental savings from confirmed containment, not merely estimated conversations.
6. Require valid experiment/control approach where feasible and signed-off finance assumptions.

## Formula sheet (no fabricated ROI numbers)
- Eligible contacts = total inbound contacts x share of safe, supported intents.
- Validated containment = eligible contacts x verified proportion resolved without a later human contact.
- Gross avoided cost = validated containment x marginal avoidable cost per contact.
- Net monthly value = gross avoided cost + validated incremental value - (voice usage + telephony + integration amortization + QA + operations).
- ROI = net benefit / total incremental cost; define horizon and confidence interval.
- Time to value = days from pilot onboarding to first owner-approved successful outcome.

## Pilot gate
Before claiming customer value, validate safety, quality, latency, cost, customer complaints, opt-out, duplicate contacts and fallback accuracy. Do not equate average call duration reductions with real staffing savings without workforce and finance validation.

## Executive readout (slide outline)
1. Situation and approved scope.
2. Existing workflow and customer friction.
3. Voice agent prototype and human controls.
4. Pilot acceptance criteria and risk management.
5. Baseline metrics and transparent value hypothesis.
6. Owners, timeline and commercial decision.
