# Voice AI Customer Adoption Playbook — UAE enterprise demonstration

**This is a proposed operating model, not an actual ElevenLabs engagement.**

## 0-30 days: Activate and demonstrate time-to-value
- Customer discovery: which existing journeys produce repeat contacts, avoidable hold time or poor customer experience?
- Baseline current duration, repeat contacts, cost, complaint categories and supported languages.
- Define one restricted voice agent use case with a named IT, legal, security and business owner.
- Build two onboarding tracks: business admin (use-case and configuration audit) and engineer (API/tool/data boundaries).
- Run an English-language workshop; obtain qualified Arabic-language QA and facilitation from fluent specialists as needed.
- Record pilot agent configuration, FAQ provenance, safety concerns, escalation rules and success rubric.

## 31-60 days: Validate, coach and automate
- Review account activation and first successful conversations (with appropriate privacy safeguards).
- Sample transcripts only under approved retention policy, never include sensitive identifiers in external AI prompts.
- Run agent next-reply, tool-call and simulated multi-turn tests; capture failures and reproducibility.
- Offer 45-minute workshop: intents, tool boundaries, knowledge freshness, human handoff and key analytics.
- Build self-serve FAQs, short videos, sandbox checklists and implementation playbooks with reusable templates.

## 61-90 days: Expand only when quality holds
- Compare before/after metrics using stable denominators and segments, not unverified return claims.
- Expand intents and languages with native-speaker quality testing, technical review and new sign-offs.
- Identify champions in customer teams and schedule monthly peer demonstrations.
- Prioritize high-value gaps via adoption data and share validated product feedback with customer success / engineering.
- Define renewal / expansion hypotheses separately from realised revenue.

## Weekly operating cadence
Monday: analytics and quality signal review. Tuesday: account cohort and adoption barriers. Wednesday: customer education webinar, recordings and office hours. Thursday: technical/prompt audit with product specialist. Friday: improve templates and share brief KPI/issue narrative with GTM and RevOps.

## Success definitions
- Account activated: onboarding complete AND at least one approved production or pilot conversation. The synthetic dataset simplifies this to a binary field.
- Conversation success: provider-specific evaluated success / all conversations. Unknown is reported separately, not silently excluded.
- Cost per interaction: total observed spend / all conversations.
- Handoff rate: human handoffs / all conversations, not equivalent to "failed" interactions.
- Time to value: date first measurable customer-approved outcome minus date onboarding completed.
- Retention / expansion: opportunity hypotheses until CRM and finance validate signed amounts.

## Deliverable pack
One-page setup guide; sample voice-agent prompt audit; test case library; live workshop deck outline; 30-day pilot scorecard; customer value hypothesis; issue escalation and responsible AI checklist.
