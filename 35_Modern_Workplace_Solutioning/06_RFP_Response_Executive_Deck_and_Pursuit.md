# 06 | Strategic pursuit, RFP response and differentiated executive story

## Sample executive presentation — eight slides
1. "What employees experience today" — symptoms and missing measurements.
2. "What the CIO/CHRO/CFO want" — productivity, security, predictable service cost.
3. "One integrated workplace service" — end-to-end tower model.
4. "The employee journey" — ready-on-day-one and interruption case.
5. "Technology choices, not technology theatre" — Windows 365/AVD, ServiceNow, DEX and risk.
6. "Service outcomes" — transparent SLA/XLA and human accountability.
7. "Commercial and transition choices" — options, exclusions, dependencies, change.
8. "Decision" — sponsor-owned phased discovery/PoV with signed success gates.

## Differentiation
- Employee-friction and business-value measures alongside ticket SLAs.
- Source-of-truth and data-quality plans before AI and automation.
- Choice of commercial constructs instead of defaulting to a single price model.
- A DEX and self-service learning loop with human escalation.
- Transparent technical/operational dependencies, including regional field service.
- Practical licence/tenant validation before making Microsoft product promises.

## Pipeline development: proactive account discovery
Listen for acquisitions, hybrid-work changes, ITSM renewals, platform consolidation, employee digital experience issues and device refreshes. Offer a 2-week measured workplace discovery with sponsor, business case and deliverables, **without representing any current client pipeline or won bids**.

## Sales/delivery collaboration
Consultant leads business framing and value story; account lead owns commercial approvals; architecture SMEs validate design; delivery leads validate run economics; risk/security approve controls; procurement/legal approve contractual terms.
