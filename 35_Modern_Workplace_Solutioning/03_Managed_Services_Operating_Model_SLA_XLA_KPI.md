# 03 | Managed-service operations, SLA / XLA / KPI and governance

## Operating model
- **Client retained team:** strategy, policy, architecture exceptions, service ownership, procurement and financial approval.
- **Lead managed-service provider:** overall service integration, service desk, technology tower coordination, contract reporting and problem management.
- **Specialist partners:** DaaS/logistics, local field services, security and virtual desktop engineering.
- **CHRO / employee experience:** adoption, employee feedback and value outcomes.
- **Service integration (SIAM-style):** coherent service catalogue, supplier boundaries, incident ownership and multi-vendor governance.

## Service levels and experience levels are not interchangeable
- **SLA:** operational commitment such as priority-based incident response, uptime or agreed provisioning time. Each must define priority taxonomy, exclusions, hours of operation and measurement window.
- **XLA:** employee-perceived outcome such as friction-free onboarding, digital sentiment, productivity interruptions or effort to obtain help. Agree survey methodology and avoid cherry-picking positive users.
- **KPI:** broader operational metric such as knowledge article freshness, device compliance, asset integrity or Copilot quality.

## Governance cadence
Daily operational queue triage, weekly tower review, monthly service performance with employee-experience voices, quarterly strategy/value committee chaired by CIO/CHRO, commercial and privacy reviews as agreed.

## Escalation and contract guardrails
P1 / security incidents use an approved major incident bridge. Supplier escalation paths, service credits, dependency/force majeure policies, floor and ceiling commitment terms, service hours and indexation all require negotiated contracts.

## RACI (illustrative)
Client CIO: A strategy; provider service manager: R service delivery; CHRO: A employee experience; security owner: A policy; service desk lead: R incidents; DaaS partner: R hardware; finance: A savings recognition; architecture board: A target-state exceptions. One named accountable owner per decision.

**No real SLA targets, credits or survey scores have been negotiated or measured.**
