# 05 | Transition & Transformation (T&T) playbook

## Phases (illustrative 26-week plan)
| Phase | Weeks | Main deliverables | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Due diligence | 1–4 | Device/asset/license/ITSM data inventory, incumbent SLAs, employee journeys | Baseline and sponsor signoff |
| Service design | 5–8 | Service catalogue, target operating model, architecture, security and pricing review | Business, security and commercial approval |
| Mobilisation | 9–12 | Named service owners, runbooks, operational training, communications and contact channels | Service desk + field readiness |
| Pilot/cutover | 13–18 | Controlled region pilot, ITSM handoffs, issue triage, change rollback | Performance and employee acceptance |
| Stabilisation | 19–22 | Major incident practice, backlog control, experience improvement, first monthly report | No unresolved critical risk |
| Optimization | 23–26 | DEX/automation priority backlog, value baseline, phased service extension | Sponsor-approved roadmap |

## Migration workstreams
Service-desk knowledge migration, integration mapping, active incidents, device lifecycle/data cleansing, employee communication and self-service changes, identity and access handover, licence reporting and contract transition.

## Change / adoption plan
Role-based communications, impacted employee groups, managers and service desk champions. Explain new support channels and escalation, onboarding support for 30 days post-cutover and survey follow-up. Train business teams in *using* Copilot only where tenant entitlement and policy are approved.

## Engineering and vendor deliverability gates
- API and ITSM integration review, asset source-of-truth reconciliation.
- Security/privacy review and zero unresolved critical exceptions.
- Confirm regional coverage, dispatch economics, spare pool and hardware recycling.
- Define AVD vs Windows 365 licensing, image/profile management and regional connectivity where in scope.
- Agree service ownership, escalation and retained organisation.
- Pilot synthetic joiner/incident flows, failure/retry cases and rollback.

## Programme RAID
Risks: poor device inventory, incumbent knowledge loss, inconsistent ITSM priority, license mismatches, privacy oversharing, insufficient in-country field coverage. Dependencies: procurement, identity, Microsoft tenant access and technology architectures. Escalate material scope or risk through steering committee.

## Operating handoff
Versioned runbooks, service ownership, knowledge base, access and incident matrix, dashboard contract, continual improvement calendar, commercial baseline and formal acceptance report.

**No actual service migration or cutover occurred.**
