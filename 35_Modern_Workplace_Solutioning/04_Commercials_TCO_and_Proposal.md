# 04 | Commercial strategy, TCO and option comparisons

## Assumed commercial scenario
Northbridge has 4,500 employees. **All rates and costs are arbitrary modelling assumptions (AED), not DXC offers.** Microsoft licensing, cloud compute for AVD, local taxes, third-party charges, security audits and transition programmes must be separately validated.

## Three commercial models
1. **Per supported user managed service:** monthly base support, endpoint/desk/tower operating services. Clear catalogue and banding; risk of billing inactive users.
2. **Consumption-led:** base governance retainers plus verified case/device transactions. Flexible; risks volatility and inconsistent ticket categories.
3. **Outcome/hybrid:** base managed service plus limited, independently auditable outcomes (e.g., joiner readiness, employee friction). Requires fair baseline, data trust and guardrails against perverse incentives.

## Sample costing logic
- Annual recurring cost = users × per-user monthly managed rate × 12 + fixed annual governance cost.
- Transition investment = one-time discovery / migration / enablement / contingency, separately shown.
- Three-year illustrative total = 3 × annual recurring + transition, **without unvalidated inflation or discounting**.
- Sensitivity: users, per-user rate, region coverage, device fleet scope, DEX licences, AVD/W365 consumption and partner operations.
- Client-side retained cost and current incumbent exit costs must be added before stating actual TCO.

## Business case bridge
Current cost inventory: desk staff, support tickets, site hands, endpoint maintenance, devices, subscriptions, downtime assumptions, onboarding delays, security remediation and transition. Proposed cost inventory: service fee, software/third-party licences, retained governance, device acquisition and change. Compare on identical populations and periods.

**Do not count time-release as bankable savings unless finance validates incremental economic realization.**
