# 01 | Executive discovery and measurable value

## Fictional client — Northbridge Holdings
4,500 workers, 3 regions, fragmented device and service-desk operations, slow onboarding, manual asset reconciliations and inconsistent employee experience.

## Executive workshop — 90 minutes
| Time | Speaker | Key question | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–15 | CIO | Which services damage productivity, resilience and risk posture? | Technology priorities |
| 15–30 | CHRO | Where does friction occur on joiner, mover, leaver journeys? | Employee journeys |
| 30–45 | CFO | What does the workplace truly cost today? | TCO input inventory |
| 45–60 | Service leads | Which operating constraints, SLAs and contracts exist? | Baseline service map |
| 60–75 | Architecture/security | What are the policy and dependency restrictions? | Design guardrails |
| 75–90 | All | Which outcomes are worth a limited paid discovery? | Sponsors, metrics and decision |

## Stakeholder interviews
- CIO: identity, service reliability, resilience, integration and service provider accountability.
- CHRO: joiner experience, remote work, accessibility, employee sentiment, talent-productivity friction and adoption.
- CFO: cost per active user, license utilization, DaaS financing, retained internal team and transition investment.
- Procurement: incumbent contracts, exit clauses, asset ownership, pricing indexation and volume bands.
- Security/Legal: endpoint posture, jurisdictional data transfers, software approvals, privacy, third-party risk.
- Service Desk / Field Support: contact volumes, live queues, resolutions, self-service and dispatch patterns.

## Business outcome-to-solution map
| Desired outcome | Candidate solution | KPI / baseline source | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faster productive onboarding | Integrated HR joiner event -> service request -> device/app access | Ready-on-day-one rate, elapsed hours | CHRO + CIO |
| Higher employee satisfaction | DEX monitoring plus human service support | Employee digital experience survey | CHRO + service owner |
| Predictable service cost | Catalogue, ticket segmentation, device lifecycle and volume tiers | Fully-loaded cost per supported worker | CFO |
| Less downtime | Proactive endpoint/experience signals and incident management | Endpoint interruption minutes | CIO |
| Better security | Conditional access, device compliance, least privilege | Compliant endpoints and remediation time | CISO |
| Practical Copilot benefits | Approved high-frequency use cases and champions | Task accuracy, adoption, verified time released | Functional sponsors |

## Value hypothesis — no observed results
- **Experience**: fewer unresolved issues and more employees ready for work on day one.
- **Productivity**: less time lost chasing service desk; cannot infer savings from deflection alone.
- **Cost**: transparent charges, fewer unused devices/licenses, reduced duplicate operating activities after validated transition.
- **Risk**: clearer service accountability, auditable identity controls, tested disaster recovery.
- **Adoption**: business-led Copilot/automation rollout with quality review.

## Discovery output
Signed opportunity brief, incumbent contract inventory, high-level architecture, application/identity map, HR-to-IT journey, service tower baseline, risk register, site and device catalogue, outcome owner map and decision to proceed or pause.

**No actual interviews were held or business impacts measured in this case study.**
