# 07 | Digital employee experience, workplace automation and responsible Copilot

## Use case 1 — predictive support triage
A device/app-health signal or user request enters approved service platform. Rules check known service errors, device and permission scope; a knowledge response can be suggested but staff/employee always have human handoff. Outcome hypothesis: fewer avoidable interruptions and first-time correct help. Telemetry access, sensitivity and consent must be approved.

## Use case 2 — self-service joiner readiness
A verified HRIS joiner event creates an ITSM request for identity, endpoint, supported apps and service access. Each sensitive provisioning action uses approved workflow and audit. Track day-one readiness and friction, not just ticket completion.

## Use case 3 — Microsoft 365 Copilot for reporting/communication
Business managers use approved M365 work-grounded content to draft project updates and meeting action summaries, independently verify source figures, and get appropriate review before distribution. Check entitlement, permissions, Graph access and tenant-specific feature availability. Measure quality, correction time and adoption before asserting benefit.

## AI governance checklist
- Data classification, legal basis and regional handling.
- Least-privilege and human approval for write or financial/HR changes.
- Versioned source knowledge and provenance.
- Prompt injection and PII leakage tests.
- Audited self-service vs required human escalation.
- Failure handling, appeal/feedback and rollback.
- Change/knowledge owners and incident governance.

## XLA integration
Ask not "How many tickets were deflected?" but "Was the employee actually able to finish their work faster and without harm?" Couple ticket data with employee friction pulse and verified task outcome measures, controlling for sampling bias.

## What is not claimed
No real tenant access, usage reports, production AI/DEX integration, service-management automation or actual cost-savings result.
