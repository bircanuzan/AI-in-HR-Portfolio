## Executive challenge
A fictional Dubai property manager has disconnected enquiry, lease, move-in, property service and renewal processes. Leads are tracked across channels, tenant issues lack unified ownership, customer data is fragmented across CRM, ERP and property systems, and reporting does not consistently distinguish new acquisitions from retained tenants.

## Target business outcomes (hypotheses, not achieved results)
- Track inquiry-to-lease conversion, lead response, onboarding completion, first-contact resolution, work-order timeliness, retention and renewal.
- Give leasing, customer service, property operations and finance a common customer/tenant/lease view without eliminating system-level data ownership.
- Standardise prioritisation, risks, releases, adoption, cross-vendor dependencies and executive value reporting.
- Keep customer-facing automation subject to human review for contract, payment, dispute and eligibility decisions.
