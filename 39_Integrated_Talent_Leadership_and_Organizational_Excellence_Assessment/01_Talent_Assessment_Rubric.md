# Talent assessment centre — illustrative observation and rating framework

**Scenario:** Early-career programme analyst selection and development. Fictional exercise.

| Competency | Observed behavior | Exercise | Rating 1–5 | Evidence note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analysis | Checks assumptions; compares evidence | Case study | Blank | Direct observed quote/action only |
| Collaboration | Listens, integrates contributions | Group discussion | Blank | Record behaviors, not personality |
| Communication | Summarizes recommendations clearly | Presentation | Blank | Separate fact and inference |
| Judgment | Weighs competing priorities | SJT | Blank | Explain decision logic |
| Learning agility | Revises conclusion after feedback | Debrief | Blank | Record response and context |

**Anchor example:** 1 = rarely demonstrates observable behavior; 3 = demonstrates with prompts; 5 = consistently demonstrates in complex scenarios. Adapt and validate anchors for each mandate.

**Workflow:** independent observations → evidence review → calibration → report → feedback/development coaching. Document missing evidence as *not observed*, not zero competence.
