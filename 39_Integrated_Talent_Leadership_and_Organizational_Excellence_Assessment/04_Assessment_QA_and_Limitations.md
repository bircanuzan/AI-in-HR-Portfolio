# Assessment quality controls and limitations

- **Job relevance:** each competency and criterion must map to the assessment purpose.
- **Consistency:** identical core instructions, time allowances and scoring anchors per candidate cohort.
- **Evidence traceability:** record who observed what and when; distinguish observable behavior from interpretation.
- **Calibration:** investigate large assessor score differences before finalization.
- **Fairness:** review accessibility needs, adverse outcomes and unexplained scoring differences where lawful data permits; do not claim validated fairness from a small mock dataset.
- **Confidentiality:** consent, least privilege, secure retention and no public real candidate records.
- **AI use:** optional human-reviewed drafting only, without invented evidence or automated selection.
- **Reporting:** explicit confidence limits and development-oriented feedback.
- **Professional boundaries:** standardized psychological testing, official excellence award assessment or bilingual Arabic delivery require appropriate credentials, partners and/or authorization.
- **Status:** fictional portfolio demonstration, not evidence of formal certification or a delivered client engagement.
