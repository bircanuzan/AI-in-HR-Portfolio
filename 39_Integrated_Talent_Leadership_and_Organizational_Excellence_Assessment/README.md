# Project 39 — Integrated Talent, Leadership & Organizational Excellence Assessment

**Portfolio case study | Synthetic demonstration; not a completed client engagement**

## Objective
Design a consistent, defensible assessment workflow across three distinct but connected services: (1) assessment-centre competency evaluation, (2) leadership, graduate and career-transition development assessment, and (3) organizational/institutional excellence diagnostics.

## Workstream 1 — Talent and Assessment Centre Evaluation
- Build role-related competency models with observable behavioral indicators.
- Run simulated structured interviews, in-tray or situational judgment exercises, and group observation rubrics.
- Maintain assessor evidence logs, independent ratings, calibration notes and individual reports.
- Provide candidate feedback, debriefing and development coaching.
- Include student/graduate assessment and career-transition use cases.

## Workstream 2 — Leadership Assessment & Development
- Map leadership capabilities: strategic judgment, stakeholder influence, change leadership, ethics and collaboration.
- Use case exercises and behavioral event interviews with anchored ratings.
- Generate leadership-potential summaries, development priorities and a 30/60/90-day development plan.
- Use human review of any AI-assisted summarization; no automated hiring or psychometric diagnosis.

## Workstream 3 — Organizational & Institutional Excellence Assessment
- Design an evidence-based organizational self-assessment aligned conceptually with EFQM-style criteria and UAE institutional excellence practices.
- Evaluate strategic direction, stakeholder value, people capabilities, process effectiveness, operational results and governance.
- Provide evidence register, maturity gaps, prioritized improvement recommendations, and leadership debrief.
- **Not** an official EFQM, ADAEP or Bila Juhd assessment or accreditation.

## Delivery methodology
1. Scope the purpose, evaluation criteria, ethical safeguards, access rights and reporting language.
2. Define frameworks and evidence requirements.
3. Conduct structured observations/interviews and document sources.
4. Independently rate, reconcile divergence and label uncertainty.
5. Quality-assure evidence completeness, fairness, confidentiality and traceability.
6. Deliver individual and organization-level reports with clear, actionable recommendations.
7. Debrief stakeholders and develop follow-up coaching/improvement plans.

## Evidence and limitations
All examples are original, hypothetical templates. This project does not claim an assessor certification, formal psychometric test license, bilingual Arabic reports, EFQM assessor authorization, or actual government assessment delivery.
