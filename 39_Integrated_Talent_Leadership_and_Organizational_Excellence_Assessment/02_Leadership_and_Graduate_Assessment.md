# Leadership, graduate and career-transition assessment

**Three audiences:** emerging leaders; university students/new graduates; experienced professionals considering career transition.

| Audience | Purpose | Methods | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emerging leaders | Development and succession readiness | Leadership simulation; structured interview; stakeholder case | Capability profile and 90-day development plan |
| Students / graduates | Career readiness and development needs | Scenario exercises; communication tasks; strengths discussion | Individual development action plan |
| Career transition | Transferable competency mapping | Evidence-based interview; job-family skills matrix; reflective coaching | Transition capability map and career development milestones |

**Leadership criteria:** strategic thinking, ethical judgment, change adoption, influence, people development, execution.

**Sample 30/60/90-day plan:** 30: baseline and coaching goal; 60: stretch assignment with feedback; 90: behavior reassessment and development review.

Human assessors interpret context. No predictive claims, psychometric diagnosis or automatic screening.
