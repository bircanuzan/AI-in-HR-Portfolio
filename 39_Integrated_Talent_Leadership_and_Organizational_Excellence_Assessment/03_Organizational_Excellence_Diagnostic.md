# Organizational and institutional excellence diagnostic

**Illustrative UAE public-sector-style service unit; not a formal framework certification.**

| Dimension | Evidence requested | Sample diagnostic question | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy & purpose | Strategy, targets, governance minutes | Do priorities connect to measurable service outcomes? | Strategy alignment gap |
| Stakeholder value | Service feedback, customer journey | Can user complaints be traced to process improvements? | Stakeholder evidence map |
| People & capability | Competency model, development plan | Are priority capabilities assessed and developed? | Skills maturity map |
| Operations | SOPs, risk register, audit actions | Are key controls owned and tested? | Process/control maturity |
| Results & improvement | KPIs, trend data, initiatives | Do results show sustained improvement? | Prioritized improvement backlog |

**Illustrative maturity scale:** 1 ad hoc; 2 emerging; 3 defined; 4 measured; 5 improving. Scores require source evidence and assessor rationale. No official EFQM, ADAEP, or Bila Juhd scoring is asserted.

**Delivery:** document review → stakeholder interviews → evidence triangulation → gap assessment → action prioritization → leadership debrief.
