# Building Agentic AI for Full HR Lifecycle

A practical Agentic AI portfolio demonstrating how governed AI agents can support the full HR lifecycle through RAG, reasoning and orchestration, tool calling, workflow automation, human approvals and continuous evaluation.

## Portfolio Contents

This project contains 12 HR AI agents covering:

- HR Policy & Employee Services
- Recruitment Intelligence & Sourcing
- Job Drafting & Job Architecture
- Onboarding Coordination
- Talent Assessment
- Talent Management & Succession
- Performance Management
- Strategic Workforce Planning
- Compensation & Benefits Intelligence
- Organisational Restructuring & Workforce Scenarios
- People Analytics & Executive Insight
- HR Operations Automation

## Agent Architecture

Trigger → Goal → Approved Inputs/Data → Knowledge/RAG → Reasoning/Orchestration → Tools/Actions → Human Approval → Exception Handling → Output/Action → Audit Trail → Evaluation

## Responsible AI

Consequential employment decisions remain human-owned. The agents are designed to support evidence preparation, analysis, workflow coordination and decision support rather than autonomously making employment decisions.
