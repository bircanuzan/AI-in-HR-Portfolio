# Discovery and value targeting — fictional MENA strategic accounts

## Customer scenario
Three fictional strategic accounts across the UAE and Saudi Arabia have 3,000+ eligible engineers. The proposed coding-agent adoption aims to reduce low-risk, repetitive maintenance effort with quality and security guardrails. A customer sponsor must establish actual eligible tasks and reliability before any real contract/ROI claim.

## Executive + technical discovery workshop, 90 minutes
1. CIO/CTO/Engineering VP: define priorities, software lifecycle bottlenecks, developer experience, risk tolerance and business value.
2. Engineering managers: sample supported workflow types (tests, bug investigations, small refactors, approved migrations) and measure baselines.
3. Security/procurement: clarify code access, data residency, credential handling, repository policies, SSO/RBAC and review gates.
4. Delivery leadership: select pilot teams with clear eligibility, owners and instrumentation.
5. Finance: define how labor capacity may be converted to real value and what costs must be considered.

## Value baseline
Per engineering workflow: task type, developer time, success rate, rework, tool/code-review cost, escaped defects, approval time and incremental agent usage spend. Explicitly separate **eligible tasks**, **activated engineers**, **monthly consumption**, **approved completions** and **reliably measured time differences**.

## Priority decision matrix
Prioritize bounded, auditable tasks with stable tests, documented standards, clear human reviewers and code branch protection. Deprioritize high-risk payment/identity/security changes, poorly instrumented repos and autonomous pushes to production.

## Quantification template
Potential hours released = activated engineers × confirmed relevant tasks per week × 4.33 × positive minutes saved per task / 60 × independently validated quality factor × credible realization factor.

Capacity value hypothesis = potential hours × attributable loaded cost. This is **not** realized revenue or cash savings. Discount rework, training, supervision, cloud/agent charges and opportunity cost before claims. Require finance signoff for any cash savings.

## Customer account management
Maintain sponsor map, risk/issues and escalations, monthly KPI executive review, roadmap decision backlog, renewal hypothesis and opportunities for additional engineering teams only after evidence and security approvals.
